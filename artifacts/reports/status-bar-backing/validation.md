# Status-bar backing - validation

## Defect

Scrolled content was drawn under the status bar with nothing behind it, so it overlapped the clock and other status-bar items.
On an iPhone SE at the largest text size the Profile tab's "Profile" heading sat on the clock once the tab scrolled, and the Progress tab did the same at every text size.
Checking every scrolling screen found the same flaw on the Today tab (at every text size) and on the session-complete screen (at the largest text size).
None of these screens has a navigation bar: each is a full-screen `ScrollView` reaching the top safe-area edge, and a scroll view draws its content into the top inset.

## Fix

`View.statusBarBacking()` (`DesignSystem/StatusBarBacking.swift`) overlays a band of `Theme.Colors.background` exactly the height of the top safe-area inset.
It is applied to the Today, Progress and Profile tabs and to the session-complete screen.
The band is the same color the screen already sits on, so an unscrolled screen is unchanged: every unscrolled render is byte-identical to the one taken before the fix.
It takes no touches and is hidden from VoiceOver.
Screens with a navigation bar (Settings, Account, Coach, the paywall, the injury control) already get the system's own backing and were not changed.
The player and rest overlay keep their top bar between the status bar and their scroll view, and the onboarding scroll view sits below its progress bar, so neither draws under the status bar.
The modal cards (first-run explainer, Trainer choice, Coach disclosure, graduation reveal) are inset and clipped, so they do not reach it either.

## Evidence

`StatusBarBackingEvidenceTests` hosts each screen as an iPhone SE (375x667 pt with a 20 pt status-bar inset and a 49 pt tab-bar inset), at the default text size and at `.accessibility5`, in light and dark.
It renders the screen as it opens (`<screen>-<look>-top.png`), scrolls it so content passes behind the status bar, renders it again (`<screen>-<look>-scrolled.png`), and asserts that every pixel of the 20 pt status-bar band is the screen's background.
Before reading the pixels it checks, from the live accessibility tree, that at least one element's frame now reaches into the band, so a screen that cannot scroll content behind the status bar fails instead of passing on an empty band.
Profile has nothing to scroll at the default size, and only the largest text size makes the session-complete screen taller than the phone, so those two screens are checked at the largest size only.
The band's last pixel row is allowed a faint tint, because the capture's resampling bleeds a few percent of the row below into it; drawn content differs by the full contrast of that row.

Without the fix every scrolled case fails, by between 1,173 and 58,698 band pixels (Today, Progress and Profile at each look they scroll at, and the session-complete screen at the largest size).
Those renders are kept as `before-<screen>-<look>-scrolled.png`; they were captured once on the pre-fix code and no run rewrites them.
The other images are regenerated by `REPTODAY_WRITE_EVIDENCE=1`.

The running app on an iPhone SE (3rd generation) Simulator, iOS 18, at the largest accessibility text size, with the Profile tab scrolled, shows the real status bar:
