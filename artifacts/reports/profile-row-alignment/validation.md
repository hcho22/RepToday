# Profile row alignment - validation

## Defect

On the Profile tab the Coach row's title started about 6-8 pt to the right of the Account and Settings titles.
`ProfileRowLabel` gave its leading SF Symbol no fixed width, so the wider `bubble.left.and.bubble.right.fill` glyph pushed the title along.
At the largest accessibility text size the fixed 56 pt row height also clipped every title to an ellipsis ("Acco...", "...", "Setti...").

## Fix

- The glyph sits in a fixed icon column (`ProfileRowIcon.columnWidth`, 30 pt at the default size, scaled relative to `.body`), sized to the widest glyph any row uses (Coach, 28 pt at 17 pt body).
  Every title now starts at the same x.
- The icon and chevron stop growing at `ProfileRowIcon.largestTextSize` (`.accessibility1`), so at the largest sizes the title keeps the row's width.
- The row grows past 56 pt instead of clipping, and at accessibility sizes the Premium badge moves under the title.
  The glyph and chevron stay centered on the title's line rather than on the title and badge together.
  The badge stays on one line, shrinking slightly rather than hyphenating ("Premi-um") on a 375 pt wide phone.
- The taller rows no longer fit an iPhone SE at the largest text sizes, so the tab scrolls when its content is taller than the screen.
  When it fits, the content still fills the screen with the rows pinned to the bottom; the default-size renders are byte-identical to the pre-scroll ones.

The `before-` images were captured once on the pre-fix code and are kept as a static record; no run rewrites them.
The after images are regenerated by `REPTODAY_WRITE_EVIDENCE=1`.
`testEveryRowGlyphFitsTheIconColumnAtEveryTextSize` checks that every row glyph fits the column at every text size from extra small up to the icon's cap.
`testEveryRowLinesUpOnItsTitleAtTheDefaultTextSize` and `testEveryRowLinesUpOnItsTitleAtTheLargestTextSize` render the real `ProfileRowLabel` for every glyph, with and without the badge.
They assert from the live accessibility frames that every title starts at the same x (within 0.5 pt), no glyph overlaps its title, and the glyph and chevron are vertically centered on the title (within 0.5 pt).
With the fixed column frame removed, both fail: the Coach title starts 8.3 pt right of the Account title at the default size and 13.7 pt right at `.accessibility5`.
The badged rows also assert the Premium badge stays on one line, hosted at the iPhone SE row width.
`testProfileScrollsOnAnIPhoneSEAtTheLargestTextLight` and `testProfileScrollsOnAnIPhoneSEAtTheLargestTextDark` assert every element on the iPhone SE keeps the height it has with unlimited room, and that each one, the Settings row included, can be scrolled fully into view between the status bar and the tab bar.
