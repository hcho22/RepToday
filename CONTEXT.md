# CONTEXT - Rep Today domain glossary

The resolved vocabulary of the Rep Today domain: the small set of terms whose meaning is load-bearing across the product, the engine, and the docs.
This is a glossary of settled terms, not a spec - each entry points to the authoritative code or doc rather than duplicating its detail.
`AGENTS.md` is the working guide for contributors; the dated PRDs under `.claude/agent/tasks/` are the strategic source of truth; decisions that changed a term's meaning are recorded as ADRs under `docs/adr/`.

## Pillar

The training families a session draws from - `strength`, `mobility`, `primal` (`Models/Enums.swift`, `Pillar`).

**Strength is the primary pillar of every session.**
Single-focus, blend, cold-start, and Return sessions all lead with strength; mobility is **supporting** - the structural warm-up and the cooldown bookends only, never a training block and never the lead.
This is the strength-primary decision that superseded the earlier co-primary framing (see [ADR-0001](docs/adr/0001-strength-primary-sessions.md)); it is captain-accepted, and the term "co-primary" is retired for describing current behavior.
Since **US-M01** the strength lead is *structural*: the engine builds every session as Warm-Up -> Strength (-> Primal at 41-60 min) -> Cooldown, with no strength-vs-mobility split machinery. Since **US-M03** the `sitsLong` (desk-worker signal) *biases* bookend selection toward posture/hip openers - never a sizing lever, so it can never reintroduce a mobility middle block (`SessionAssembly.postureHipLean`; see AGENTS.md section 2).
Since **US-M02** the bookends are *pattern-matched*: each mobility movement carries a `complements: [MovementPattern]` tag (`Exercises.json`), and the warm-up and cooldown **lead** with a stretch complementing the day's lead strength pattern, then fill with the existing staleness / no-repeat ordering - a preference, never a filter (`SessionAssembly.leadingComplement`); the general pool is the fallback so a bookend is never starved.

`primal` (bear crawl, crab walk, ground-to-standing) is a first-class pillar: it earns its own dedicated block in extended (41-60 min) sessions and folds into the strength family in shorter blends (US-E02).
The engine mechanics live in `Services/Engine/SessionAssembly.swift` (the block builder); `AGENTS.md` section 2 ("Pillar balance") is the working description.

## Movement Practice (retired)

The mobility **training** block that once sat between the warm-up and cooldown - a real block of mobility work (deep squat holds, hip work, thoracic rotations), distinct from the structural warm-up.
**Retired by US-M01** (2026-08-13): it is no longer emitted at any length, its split machinery (`PillarBalance`/`PillarWeights`) is removed, and the training minutes it held were reallocated to strength.
Mobility now survives only as the warm-up and cooldown bookends. The term is kept here only so a future contributor recognizes it as a removed concept, not a live block (see [ADR-0001](docs/adr/0001-strength-primary-sessions.md) and the "Movement Practice removal" follow-on in `.claude/agent/tasks/prd-strength-primary-sessions_260812.md`).

## Continuous Circuit (planned)

The **planned/target** active-session model: a hands-free, follow-along session that auto-advances on a per-interval countdown - the strength block runs as circuit rounds ("Round N of M"), each work window counts down and auto-flows into rest with no tap, and warm-up/cooldown holds auto-start and flow linearly.
It is **partially built** (see the story-by-story status below): it **supersedes** the manual tap-to-advance player (the US-K01/US-K02/US-O03 model where every set needs a **Complete set** tap and every stretch a **Start hold** tap) as each slice lands, and is never user-selectable alongside it - what has not landed yet still falls back to the manual path rather than to a second mode.
The authoritative spec is the Continuous-Circuit Sessions PRD (`.claude/agent/tasks/prd-continuous-circuit-sessions_260814.md`, `US-CC##`); the two load-bearing decisions behind it are recorded as [ADR-0002](docs/adr/0002-per-interval-pacer-clock.md) (the per-interval **pacer clock**, which partially reverses US-O03's hidden-clock stance) and [ADR-0003](docs/adr/0003-even-round-circuit-timing.md) (**even-round** timing: a uniform set count per training block, with the between-round rest as the fit lever that replaces per-exercise set adjustment).
Reps stay the currency (no timed-interval conversion); the deterministic engine's progression and Adaptive Overload are unchanged - this is a player change plus the one even-round timing-model change in `SessionAssembly`.
Self-pacing is preserved by in-flow escape hatches (chiefly **+ More time**), never a parallel manual mode.
**Partially landed:** US-CC01 shipped the first player slice - a rep-based **strength/primal** training set now counts down on an auto-advancing **work window** (`ActiveSessionViewModel.workWindow`, `ActiveSessionView.WorkWindowCountdownView`) that records the set at zero and flows into the *existing* US-K02 rest with no tap, plus a prominent **Done** to advance early; the window reads the engine's already-planned per-set seconds (`SessionAssembly.workSecondsPerSet`). **US-CC03/US-CC04 have landed** (engine-only, ADR-0003): `SessionAssembly` now sizes a training block as a circuit of a **uniform round count** (every station's `sets` equal) and lands the ±1 min target by tuning a **bounded between-round rest** (30-75s) as the primary fit lever, with a fixed ~15s between-station transition as the distinct second gap - the per-exercise set-adjust lever is retired (`SessionAssembly.blockSeconds`/`setRoundsAndRest`). **US-CC02 has landed** (player-only): the strength / dedicated-primal training block is played as a **circuit of rounds** - the player rotates one set of each station per round (A, B, C, A, B, C, ...) via `ActiveSessionViewModel.nextPosition(fromIndex:set:)` and surfaces **"Round N of M"** (`currentRound`/`circuitRoundCount`), warm-up/cooldown bookends stay linear (no round), and aggregate logging is unchanged. **US-CC05 has landed** (player-only): a warm-up/cooldown bookend **hold auto-starts hands-free** (never a Start-hold tap) and a per-side bookend flows side 1 -> a brief **"Switch sides"** beat -> side 2 with no tap (`ActiveSessionViewModel.autoStartHoldIfNeeded`/`currentStepIsBookendHold`), while a timed strength/primal *training* hold keeps the US-O03 manual Start-hold path. **US-CC06 has landed** (player-only): the quiet in-flow escape hatches with **no separate manual mode** - the new one is an explicit user **Pause** (`ActiveSessionViewModel.pause(asOf:)`/`resume(asOf:)`, a Pause/Resume toggle in the shared `ActiveSessionView.SessionTopBar`) that freezes whichever countdown is live - work window, hold, or rest - and its audio-cue timing, then resumes from the exact remainder, **without backgrounding** (a foreground state kept distinct from the scene-phase background pause via `pauseForBackground`/`resumeFromForeground`); the other hatches (Done, Skip, Swap, round-rest +/skip) are surfaced as-is, and `NoManualModeGuardTests` fails the build if a `manualMode`/`ManualMode` identifier ever appears in the player sources. **US-CC07 has landed** (player-only, a hardening/lock-in story with no engine change): a mid-circuit **skip** or **swap** holds for **all remaining rounds** and the block stays structurally even - both were already structural once US-CC02/US-CC03 landed (the player keeps one `Step` per station, so skip passes over the station in every round and a swap replacing the slot carries the substitute through all later rounds while `ExerciseSwap` keeps the block's uniform round count M), and the settled product decision is **Option A, honest late-entrant**: a swap's substitute joining in round *r* logs only the `M-r+1` rounds it actually played rather than fabricating a pre-swap set. **US-CC08 has landed** (engine-only): `SessionAssembly.workSecondsPerSet` is no longer a planning-only proxy but a **generous runtime pace** - one documented constant `SessionAssembly.workPaceGenerosityFactor = 1.25` applied *inside* that function, so the same inflated number feeds both the planning wall-clock and the on-screen work window and the window can never be roomier than what the fit planned. It scales the **rep branch only** (a hold's per-second cost is definitional, not estimated, so holds pass through at 1.0) and never touches Step 6's per-set target. Two accepted, measured consequences: a session fits **fewer rounds** - and at 45 min one fewer **station** (8 rounds x 4 instead of 7 x 5, so the longest strength block trains 4 distinct movements) - with a fast user finishing early and tapping **Done**; and the US-M03 `sitsLong` bookend bias now genuinely **binds** on the shipped catalog, so its byte-identical-training-middle guard was rescoped to the real invariant (same movements at the same per-set targets, blocks still internally even, equal bookend seconds still producing identical round counts). The rest of the model - the work-window/hold **+ More time** extension (US-CC14, only rest `extendRest` is surfaced) and the migration/explainer/full-a11y stories - is **not yet built**, so a non-bookend timed *training* hold and non-training sets still use the manual player. Do not read the fuller description above as current beyond the strength work window, its circuit rotation with skip/swap holding across rounds, the hands-free bookends, the user Pause and its sibling escape hatches, and the engine's even-round sizing at the generous pace.

## discipline-first

The product's positioning: the promise is **daily consistency** - showing up - independent of whether a given day's work is strength or mobility.
Discipline-first is a framing about the user's behavior and identity ("you're someone who moves"), not a claim about the session's pillar mix.
It coexists with the strength-primary engine: the engine leads with strength while the positioning stays discipline-first, and every user starts in the **Discipline Phase** and earns the **Strength Phase** through sustained consistency plus demonstrated competence (`Services/Consistency/`, `PhaseEvaluator`; `AGENTS.md` "Consistency & Phase").
No gamification (no XP, levels, badges, or a streak to break); a miss dents but never zeroes the Consistency Score.
