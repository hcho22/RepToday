# ADR-0015: A Practice Movement bypasses the staple gate

- Status: Accepted (decided 2026-11-10); not yet implemented (M1, `US-TD02`, `US-TD04`).
- Date: 2026-11-10
- Deciders: captain, via the Push/Pull/Legs design grill (2026-11-09 to 2026-11-10)
- Spec: `.claude/agent/tasks/prd-ppl-rest-accounts-coach-agent_261110.md`
- Amends: [ADR-0007](0007-staple-movements-for-beginner-and-intermediate.md) - one narrow exception to "the only kind a beginner or intermediate user is offered".
- Relates to: `CONTEXT.md` -> "Practice Movement", "Staple movement".

## Context

A 60-minute Push or Pull Day serves four main movements, but each group has only two progression ladders, and progression wins over variety (each ladder contributes its current rung).
The captain chose to fill slots 3 and 4 with the next harder rung at low reps.
For many users that rung sits above their staple level (Diamond for a beginner, Pike for an intermediate), so the staple gate would empty the slot.

## Decision

**A Practice Movement - the next harder rung, at 4x10 (per side 4x5, holds 15s) - may be served above the user's staple level.**
The exception covers the staple gate only: injury exclusions, Strength-Phase locks and withdrawn movements still apply.
With no harder rung available, the rung below fills the slot at volume reps; with neither, the session has fewer movements.
A Practice Movement never advances the ladder, Earned Reps, or foundation clearing.

## Considered Options

- Rung below as a volume movement (default) - rejected by the captain in favor of previewing progression.
- Leaving the slot empty - rejected.

## Consequences

- The movement-access decision gains a second, practice-specific entry point; it remains decided in one place.
- When Variations get Trainer art they extend or replace Practice slots.
