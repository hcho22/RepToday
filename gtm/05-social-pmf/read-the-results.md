# How to read the results (one page, binding)

## What a win looks like at zero followers

A win is RELATIVE, never absolute.
The signal is the rank ordering of posts on their pre-registered metrics once >=10 posts have shipped and cleared the 200-impression floor.
A real win looks like: one angle's posts repeatedly landing top quartile on their pre-registered signal across two or more weekly reads, on both platforms, while sibling angles from the same bank do not.
Consistency across the pair of platforms is stronger evidence than a single spike on one.
Comment content is a first-class result at this scale: strangers describing their own streak grief or paywall anger under a post is qualitative PMF signal that a like count cannot fake.
A single viral outlier is NOT a win for its angle until a re-cut of the same angle also ranks; one post can luck into a test audience.

## What is noise at this sample size

- Single-digit engagement on early posts concludes nothing, in either direction.
- Any post under 200 impressions is unread, not failed.
- Differences of a few rank positions between two posts are noise; only large, repeated rank gaps (top quartile vs bottom quartile, held across two reads) adjudicate anything.
- Sequential A/B legs are confounded by time; a pair resolves only when the rank gap is large AND both legs were readable, otherwise it reposts before judgment.
- Follower counts, likes from friends, and week-one subscriber movement are vanity at this scale and enter no decision rule.
- Absence of comments is not negative sentiment; at low reach, most posts get zero comments regardless of quality.

## Which results change the PRODUCT, not the copy

These are pre-registered so they cannot be rationalized away later:

1. If forgiveness angles (P02, P03, P13) win big while ready-on-open angles (P01, P14, P15) flop across both platforms and two windows, the wedge story is backwards: the emotional core is the forgiving score, and readiness is the supporting proof.
   That reorders the messaging hierarchy and arguably the onboarding-free flow's emphasis, not just captions.
2. The inverse (readiness wins, forgiveness flops) confirms Hero A's primacy and demotes Hero B to a supporting pillar.
3. If P08 (mobility as relief) is a repeated top-quartile performer, mobility deserves louder placement in the product's first-session experience and the listing subtitle conversation reopens.
4. If P16-style engine explanations draw disproportionate substantive comments, the honest-architecture story is an acquisition asset and the build-in-public channel deserves real investment.
5. If comments repeatedly ask for a capability the product refuses on purpose (streaks, social features, physique tracking), that is recorded as observed demand and answered honestly; it does NOT trigger a product change by itself, because the refusals are the thesis.
6. If NOTHING clears the floor, the single escalation ladder applies, stated identically here, in investment-thesis.md K0, and in channel-plan.md A1.
   Day 14, the midpoint gate: kill the losing angles and rebuild the concept matrix once from what the midpoint taught.
   The queued angles (AB-4, AB-5, P05, P06, P12, P13, P15, P16) are the seed pool for that one rebuild.
   Week 8, if no angle has cleared its pre-registered floor over readable posts (at least 200 impressions): bet (c) is revised, not failed; the organic-first channel thesis is weakened, rebuilt from Reddit-listening input, and a second 14-day window runs on the rebuilt matrix.
   Week 16, if there is still no signal: K0 trips, bet (c) is declared failed, and that is the walk-away observation for the marketing side.

## The honest n-too-small caveats

This kit cannot prove PMF; at best it ranks messages by resonance among strangers.
Two 14-day windows on two platforms is a message-market screen, not a market test: no downloads exist to measure, no retention exists to measure, and resonance with scrollers is not evidence anyone will use the product.
Every threshold in this kit (the 200-impression floor, top/bottom quartile, two consecutive reads) is [ASSUMPTION] a pre-committed judgment call chosen to resist wishful reinterpretation, not a statistically derived power calculation; with n this small, discipline about not over-reading beats any formula.
The strongest possible outcome here is a ranked shortlist of angles that earned a second, bigger test, plus mined vocabulary from real comments.
The results are recorded in `creative-log.json` whether they are flattering or not, and a null result is a recordable result.
