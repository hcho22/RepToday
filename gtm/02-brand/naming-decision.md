# Brand + Naming Decision (v2)

## Name verdict: **RepToday stands** (stress-tested, not grandfathered)

Listing name: **"Rep Today"** (9 chars; the v1 suffix "Rest Tomorrow" is killed, see below). Subtitle: **"Opens to a ready workout"** (24 chars, carried from v1 D-005/D-009).

> **Clearance status - read this first.**
> Formal trademark clearance (USPTO or any registry) and App Store Connect name reservation are **UNVERIFIED** for Rep Today and for every candidate considered.
> Nothing in this document is a clearance claim.
> A USPTO search (ideally with counsel) and an App Store Connect name reservation are **the founder's next action** before this name is final.
> The defensibility judge adds: Rep Today is a descriptive/suggestive mark with a narrow enforcement perimeter; the founder accepts that in exchange for a name that is itself the product's call to action.

## How the v2 verdict was reached

The v2 prompt changed the naming task from "propose a name" to "stress-test RepToday and either confirm it or beat it", with incumbency explicitly not a score.
Each of the four blind positioning pitchers independently stress-tested RepToday under their own lead against the live alternates (Cairn, Stack) and was free to propose one new challenger.
All four confirmed RepToday and none proposed a challenger.
All three judges then issued independent name verdicts on top of the pitches plus the v1 collision research.
All three: RepToday stands.

The recurring reasons, across seven independent verdicts:
- Zero decode cost for a cold scroller; the name is the product's call to action.
- The cleanest collision scan of any candidate (see findings below).
- Cairn and Stack both carry accumulation/stacking metaphors that read streak-shaped, against the product's own forgiveness mechanics, on top of their collisions.

## The one material naming change: the listing suffix dies

All four pitchers and all three judges independently flagged the v1 listing name **"Rep Today, Rest Tomorrow"**: under hostile paraphrase it reads as grind-culture ("no rest until tomorrow") or as prescribing rest, and it fights the forgiveness pillar.
**[DECIDED BY AGENT]** The listing name becomes plain **"Rep Today"**, with the mechanic wedge staying in the subtitle ("Opens to a ready workout").
Rejected alternatives: pitch-3's "Rep Today: Showing Up Counts" (emotional-contract register the judges scored below mechanic register) and a keyword suffix (rejected for D-005's original reasons: it would optimize an unmeasured volume guess and spend the name field on words the keyword field already carries).
Revisit with real ASO tooling post-launch; listing names are changeable, bundle ids are not.

### The discipline question (the v2 brief's own test)

The v2 brief made discipline the proposed spine and required a genuine anti-discipline pitch.
The anti-discipline pitch won, and all three judges converged without coordination on the same disposition: **discipline is the internal spine only, never the surface lead word** (in-market prior is drill-sergeant; absent from user pain vocabulary; needs a chaperone sentence that 6-second formats delete).
Reported honestly per the brief's own instruction: the discipline lead lost on the scorecard.
The inversion sentence ("the app is the disciplined one") survives at essay length and as exactly one pre-registered PMF test angle.

### Why the losers lost

- **pitch-1 (discipline inversion):** the word "discipline" is live-owned by the opposite register (Jillian Michaels "no-excuses" creative), and the inversion collapses to the drill-sergeant reading under 6-second compression; soft overpromise drift ("the app carries the discipline for you").
- **pitch-3 (showing up):** strongest hook in the field ("congratulated me for coming back"), but its headline had a word-level collision with a live competitor ad, one line was an unenforceable experience guarantee, and one line was a false data-practices claim; rebuilt, it ships as Hero B.
- **pitch-4 (contrarian refusal):** maximum register distance but the cheapest words to copy ("no streaks"), plus an unsubstantiable competitor-intent claim that had to be pulled inside; survives as one PMF angle, mechanics only.

### What the winner grafts

- From pitch-3: the forgiveness pillar promoted to #3 and the entire Hero B variant.
- From pitch-1: the essay-layer inversion and one PMF test angle.
- From pitch-4: one contrarian PMF angle, restricted to our own mechanics.

## Name findings (from [name-collisions.md](../01-research/name-collisions.md), fetched 2026-07-15; key URLs re-verified live 2026-08-01)

- **Rep Today** - cleanest candidate. No same-name or close-variant app in a US App Store search for "rep today"; the crowded "Rep"-prefix space is gym-logging trackers, none using "Today". Main web collision: REP Fitness, a home-gym *equipment* retailer. reptoday.app returned NXDOMAIN; github.com/reptoday 404 (availability signals); X/Instagram handles unverifiable (login walls). reptoday.com is parked at HugeDomains, $3,895 buy-now - purchase deferred by the founder.
- **Cairn** - rejected: live same-category collision ("Cairn - Hiking Safety Tracker", Health & Fitness) plus the acquired-and-renamed Cairn outdoor-box brand history; and (new in v2) the stacked-stones metaphor reads streak-shaped against the forgiveness mechanics.
- **Stack** - rejected: exact-name Ketchapp game with 56K+ ratings, "Stack Team App" in Sports, Stack Sports owning web mindshare; and (new in v2) accumulation semantics are counter-positioned to a score that forgives.
- **FitSnack** (abandoned) - the rebrand remains validated: a live App Store fitness app brands itself "FITsnack" with "daily micro home workouts".
- Previously eliminated for collisions and not re-run: Groundwork, Keystone, Keizoku.

## Known risks, carried visibly

1. **Descriptive-mark weakness** - "Rep" and "Today" are common words; enforcement perimeter is narrow and a coined name would have stronger trademark posture. Accepted trade-off, re-affirmed in v2.
2. **REP Fitness owns "rep" web mindshare** - an equipment brand, not an app; the discovery bet is App Store search, not web SEO.
3. **Trademark totally unverified** - see the caveat at top. This is the founder's next action, before any listing name is typed into App Store Connect.
