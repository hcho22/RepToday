# Brand + Naming Decision

## Name recommendation: keep **Rep Today**

Listing name: **"Rep Today, Rest Tomorrow"** (24 chars). Subtitle: **"Opens to a ready workout"** (24 chars).

> **Clearance status - read this first.**
> Formal trademark clearance (USPTO or any registry) and App Store Connect name reservation are **UNVERIFIED** for Rep Today and for every candidate considered.
> Nothing in this document is a clearance claim.
> A USPTO search (ideally with counsel) and an App Store Connect name reservation are **the founder's next action** before this name is final.

## How the decision was made

A blind tournament: four independent pitch agents, each locked to a different strategic angle, produced a full positioning + name + hero message without seeing each other's work.
Three judges with different published rubrics (differentiation, conversion, defensibility/legal) scored all four.
Full pitches and scorecards are in [`tournament/`](tournament/).

### Scorecard (weighted totals, 0-10)

| Pitch | Angle | Hero headline | Differentiation | Conversion | Defensibility | Mean |
|-------|-------|---------------|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **pitch-1 (winner)** | Friction as the enemy | "Open the app. Your workout is already there." | **7.55** | **8.65** | **8.85** | **8.35** |
| pitch-4 | Audience-first | "A floor, a wall, and five minutes." | 6.70 | 7.90 | 8.55 | 7.72 |
| pitch-2 | Identity/discipline | "Become someone who moves every day." | 6.45 | 5.60 | 8.65 | 6.90 |
| pitch-3 | Anti-fitness-app | "The fitness app for people who hate fitness apps." | 6.25 | 7.50 | 7.10 | 6.95 |

The win was unanimous: all three judges ranked pitch-1 first.
The visible margin: +0.63 mean over the runner-up, and first place on every rubric.

### Why the losers lost

- **pitch-4 (audience-first):** most ownable hero line in the field, but its "Rep Today: 5-Min Home Workouts" listing name narrows a 5-60 minute product for years, and the differentiation judge found its claimed quadrant partially occupied (the live "FITsnack"-branded micro-workout app).
- **pitch-2 (identity):** best trademark posture and the most durable identity frame, but the conversion judge scored its headline category-ambiguous to a cold stranger and unprovable at first run, and its "Seven/Peloton/Freeletics punish a missed day" comparison was the largest unsubstantiated-claim exposure on the table.
- **pitch-3 (anti-fitness-app):** punchiest hook, but the defensibility judge found an invented notification-behavior claim and named-competitor attributes the research does not support, on anti-streak ground that HN evidence shows is already contested.

### What the winner grafts from the losers

- From pitch-2: "Missing a day never zeroes you out" ships as the forgiveness pillar's sample line (pitch-1 itself flagged that it under-spent on forgiveness).
- From pitch-4: "A floor, a wall, and five minutes" ships as the package's secondary line for social/ads.
- From pitch-3: nothing verbatim; its energy survives in the negative space ("No questions" / "no account") already in the winner's subhead.

## Name findings (from [name-collisions.md](../01-research/name-collisions.md), all fetched 2026-07-15)

- **Rep Today** - cleanest candidate. No same-name or close-variant app in a US App Store search for "rep today"; the crowded "Rep"-prefix space is gym-logging trackers, none using "Today". Main web collision: REP Fitness, a home-gym *equipment* retailer. reptoday.app returned NXDOMAIN (strong availability signal, registry-level confirmation not obtained); github.com/reptoday 404 (available signal); X/Instagram handles unverifiable (login walls). reptoday.com is parked at HugeDomains, $3,895 buy-now - purchase deferred by the founder.
- **Cairn** - rejected: live same-category collision ("Cairn - Hiking Safety Tracker", Health & Fitness), plus the acquired-and-renamed Cairn outdoor-box brand history; github.com/cairn taken.
- **Stack** - rejected: exact-name Ketchapp game with 56K+ ratings, "Stack Team App" in Sports, Stack Sports owning web mindshare; search discoverability would be very poor.
- **FitSnack** (abandoned) - the rebrand is validated: a live App Store fitness app brands itself "FITsnack" and describes "daily micro home workouts", nearly the same concept.

All four blind pitchers independently recommended keeping Rep Today - none judged the alternatives or a new coinage worth the switching cost of redoing the completed identifier migration (`com.reptoday.app` bundle, CloudKit container, StoreKit ids), which is free in money but not in effort or risk, pre-submission.

## Known risks, carried visibly

1. **"Rest Tomorrow" can misread** - the defensibility judge flagged that the pun could be read as prescribing rest tomorrow (it actually names the reward of showing up today). Mitigation: the subtitle "Opens to a ready workout" does the literal work; monitor comprehension in the first creator/community reactions and be willing to change the listing name (listing names are changeable; bundle ids are not).
2. **REP Fitness owns "REP" web mindshare** - an equipment brand, not an app, but SEO for "rep" terms will be contested. The app's discovery bet is App Store search, not web search.
3. **Trademark totally unverified** - see the caveat at top. "Today" and "Rep" are common words; a coined name would have a stronger trademark posture (pitch-2's analysis). The founder accepts descriptive-name risk in exchange for a name that is itself the product's call to action.
