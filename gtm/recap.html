What Rep Today is

A zero-equipment, bodyweight-only micro-workout iOS app for busy, desk-bound adults - the tired parent at 9pm, not the gym rat. The app opens to a complete, pre-generated 5-to-60-minute session with one dominant Start button; it never asks a question between opening and starting. Sessions are built on-device, deterministically, offline, in under 100 milliseconds per the PRD engine spec (a real-device benchmark is a pre-publication gate and has not yet been run). The Consistency Score is rolling and forgiving, not a streak: a 5-minute session is a full show-up, a missed day dents the score but never zeroes it, and a return after a gap is celebrated. No XP, levels, badges, or leaderboards anywhere. Free tier is unlimited workouts forever; Premium (~$7.99/mo or ~$59.99/yr, 14-day trial, planned pricing) adds depth, never access. Status: MVP core loop implemented, 879/879 tests passing, not yet submitted to the App Store. Source of truth: the product facts brief.

The channel verdict

Run zero paid spend pre-launch; build first signal from organic short-form content and a first-party waitlist. The verdict is structural, not thrift: install ads cannot run at all today (Apple Ads and Meta app campaigns both require a live, registered app), and even post-launch, tiny budgets buy noise - iOS install measurement is aggregated, delayed, and partly modeled, while organic-plus-waitlist measurement is first-party, immediate, and unthrottled by any attribution framework. The doctrine on top: creative carries the targeting, the market picks the message (not the founder), and cadence beats a launch week.

Ranking A - founder with $0 and no audience, in order:

  1. A1. Organic short-form: TikTok primary, YouTube Shorts secondary, running the PMF kit matrix.
  2. A2. App Store search / ASO preparation for launch day.
  3. A3. Reddit: listening channel plus the one sanctioned venue.
  4. A4. Personal network and the waitlist.
  5. A5. Creator seeding by honest DM, at zero cost.

The week-one instrument: the PMF kit

The PMF kit at 05-social-pmf/ is the thesis's cheapest falsification instrument and the first thing that runs: 16 pre-registered angles (each with a hypothesis, a success signal, and a kill criterion fixed before posting), 6 hook-only A/B pairs, a 14-day cadence with a fixed mid-point review gate, and a read-the-results protocol that names what is noise at zero-follower sample sizes. K0, the pre-launch kill signal, reads directly off this kit. Files: angles, A/B pairs, 14-day cadence, platform assignment, read the results, week-1 drafts, creative log.

The thesis, next to the strongest objection against it

The bet (investment thesis v2)

Rep Today matters only if four things are true at once: (a) session-start friction is a real churn driver in fitness apps; (b) a product that opens ready, forgives missed days, and keeps unlimited workouts free forever converts the tired non-athletes the category ignores; (c) one solo founder with zero ad budget can reach them through organic short-form running a pre-registered message matrix, a first-party waitlist, one-shot launch posts, and App Store search; (d) retention economics carry the premium conversion, which requires retaining meaningfully above category medians and word of mouth compounding. If any one fails, the company fails, and the kill criteria are designed to detect which one failed within 90 days of launch.

Ten kill criteria: K0 (pre-launch message signal, adjudicated over readable posts with a named "under-sampled" state) plus K1-K8 (first session, D7, D30, weekly active exercisers, organic installs, trial starts, free-to-paid, wedge comprehension) and K9 (post-launch word-of-mouth direction). The K0 ladder: day 14, kill losing angles and rebuild the matrix once; week 8 with no angle cleared, bet (c) is revised and rebuilt from Reddit-listening input for one more 14-day window; week 16 with still no signal, K0 trips, bet (c) is declared failed - the walk-away observation for the marketing side. Full document: investment-thesis.md; one-page version: investor teaser.

The strongest surviving objection (red team v2)

The wedge is copyable at the only layer the audience sees. From the competitor persona, shipped undefeated in the dossier:

Positioning.md already concedes "perceived instant-open can be faked with caching," and that concession is the whole counter-playbook: an incumbent ships a "Today's pick, ready when you open" card that server-caches one recommended class overnight, then runs the identical screen-record format with an instructor's face on it. A scroller cannot distinguish deterministic on-device generation from a cached recommendation in 8 seconds, and the incumbent's version carries "4.9 stars, 813K ratings" where Rep Today's carries "not released yet." Only free-forever and no-streaks are structurally safe, and a moat that narrow must carry the whole company.

It is ranked strongest because it has a named actor with a live adjacent ad flight, a concrete one-quarter mechanism, and no possible copy fix; it attacks the hero claim itself, not the packaging. The v2 red team produced 58 findings (26 MUST-FIX, 16 SURVIVING-OBJECTION, 16 NOTE); every one is dispositioned in the dossier, none silently dropped.
