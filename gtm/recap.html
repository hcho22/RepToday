The business, in under five minutes

The product. Rep Today is a discipline-first micro-workout iOS app. Open it and a complete zero-equipment bodyweight session is already on screen, generated on the phone, offline, deterministically, in under 100 milliseconds. One dominant Start button; no questions, no account, no paywall before the first rep. Sessions run 5 to 60 minutes across three pillars: bodyweight strength, mobility (co-primary), and primal movement. An AI Programmer tunes each user's session policy between sessions; it never generates a workout and is never in the way of Start. The Consistency Score is a forgiving rolling average, not a streak: a 5-minute session counts in full, a missed day dents but never zeroes, and a return after a gap is celebrated. No XP, levels, badges, or leaderboards anywhere. Free tier: unlimited workouts forever. Premium (planned $7.99/mo, $59.99/yr, 14-day trial) unlocks depth, never workouts.

The status, honestly. Pre-launch. Zero users, zero downloads, zero revenue, zero testimonials. What exists: an implemented MVP (667/667 tests green, StoreKit 2, CloudKit, HealthKit, Sign in with Apple), a completed rebrand to Rep Today with permanent identifiers locked, and this package.

The bet. Session-start friction and punishing gamification are real, citable churn drivers in fitness apps; a ready-on-open, forgiving, free-core product converts the tired non-athletes the category ignores; and a solo founder can reach them with zero ad budget through ASO whitespace, communities, and creators. The research found no competitor that combines instant-open, offline generation, a non-streak score, and an ungated free core. The full case, for and against, with kill criteria, is in the investment thesis.

Name recommendation

Rep Today  App Store listing name: "Rep Today, Rest Tomorrow", subtitle "Opens to a ready workout".

Chosen by a blind 4-pitch tournament scored by 3 judges with different published rubrics; the win was unanimous and all four pitchers independently kept the incumbent name. Cairn (live same-category App Store collision) and Stack (saturated) were rejected. Scorecard and losers: naming decision.

Unverified clearance. Formal trademark search and App Store Connect name reservation were NOT performed and cannot be from this environment. Both are the founder's next action. Nothing in this package is a clearance claim.

The thesis says

The wedge is real and citable: users ask verbatim for zero-decision workout apps, streak loss makes people quit, and hard paywalls are hated in-category. No researched competitor combines Rep Today's four mechanics. The category monetizes well per install, and the falsification plan is cheap: a TestFlight cohort and 90 days of measurement against eight pre-committed kill criteria.

The red team answers

"Nothing in this product is defensible. Every leg of the wedge, a ready session on open, a forgiving non-streak score, and an ungated free tier, is a UX or pricing choice with no data moat, no network effect, and no switching cost behind it; Seven or Bend could copy the entire stack in one release cycle the moment it shows signs of working. The quadrant is empty of competitors, but it is also empty of barriers, and a fully successful 90-day experiment proves a feature set, not a company."

That objection survived the rewrite and ships unanswered, alongside 29 others. The red team also forced material changes: raised kill thresholds, recomputed base-case economics, a legal line ruled false and replaced across six assets, and a rebuilt video. How this was made

Orchestration topology: a 14-agent research fan-out (per-competitor teardowns, review mining, economics, ASO, creators, name collisions), a blind 4-pitch positioning tournament judged by 3 rubric-published judges, a fresh-agent brand gate test, 3 parallel builders (site, video, thesis) plus 4 extras builders, and a 5-persona red team (adversarial skeptic, seed investor, competitor growth head, cynical target user, App Store reviewer/FTC lawyer) whose objections forced rewrites recorded in the dossier and decisions log. Every factual market claim in the package carries a URL fetched during the run; guesses are labeled [ASSUMPTION]; the app's pre-launch reality (zero users) is stated wherever a stranger might otherwise assume traction.

Pre-launch. "Rep Today" has not been trademark-searched or registered, and the App Store name has not been reserved - both are the founder's next action. Nothing in this package has been published, deployed, or purchased.
