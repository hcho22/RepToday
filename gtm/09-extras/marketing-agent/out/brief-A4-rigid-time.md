# Creative brief: A4-rigid-time

Status: draft.
This brief was generated by the dry-run creative loop.
It has never been published and has never touched a real ad account.

## Pain state

Prescribed sessions do not fit the time you actually have and cannot be adjusted.
Verbatim source quote: "trying to finish up in 30-40 minutes what the app would have you take 60 minutes to do"
Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/freeletics-workouts-fitness/id654810212

## Hypothesis

People squeezed by fixed 60-minute plans will respond to a 5-minute floor that still counts.

## Hook

Five minutes on a hotel-room floor still counts.

## Format

static card, hook plus 5-60 minute range shown in UI (instagram)

## Pre-registered success signal

saves per 1,000 impressions beats the angle-bank median over 7 days

## Kill criterion

bottom-quartile saves per 1,000 impressions across two consecutive 7-day reads

## Disclosure

Screen images simulated. App is pre-release.
