# Creative brief: A3-decision-fatigue

Status: draft.
This brief was generated by the dry-run creative loop.
It has never been published and has never touched a real ad account.

## Pain state

Deciding what to do before a workout is the hardest part; excessive scrolling and clicks before anything starts.
Verbatim source quote: "I need the app to tell me exactly what to do"
Source: https://hn.algolia.com/api/v1/items/36666806

## Hypothesis

People with decision fatigue will respond to a demo where the session already exists at app open.

## Hook

Open the app. Your workout is already there.

## Format

8s screen-record video, cold open to ready session (tiktok)

## Pre-registered success signal

watch-through to the Start tap above account median over 7 days

## Kill criterion

watch-through below account median on two consecutive 7-day reads

## Disclosure

Screen images simulated. App is pre-release.
