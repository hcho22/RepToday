# Creative brief: A2-streak-grief

Status: draft.
This brief was generated by the dry-run creative loop.
It has never been published and has never touched a real ad account.

## Pain state

Losing a streak through no fault of your own resets you to zero and makes you quit the app entirely.
Verbatim source quote: "That was it, back to 0, through no fault of my own."
Source: https://hn.algolia.com/api/v1/items/40903998

## Hypothesis

People who quit apps after an unfair reset will respond to a forgiving rolling score with nothing to lose.

## Hook

Missing a day never zeroes you out.

## Format

12s screen-record video, missed day then unchanged score (tiktok)

## Pre-registered success signal

3-second hold rate above account median over 7 days

## Kill criterion

hold rate below account median on two consecutive 7-day reads

## Disclosure

Screen images simulated. App is pre-release.
