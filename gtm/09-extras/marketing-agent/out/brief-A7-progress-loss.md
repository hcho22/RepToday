# Creative brief: A7-progress-loss

Status: draft.
This brief was generated by the dry-run creative loop.
It has never been published and has never touched a real ad account.

## Pain state

Cloud load and sync failures destroy saved progress and stretch sessions past their stated length.
Verbatim source quote: "Not being able to save your workout if you quit halfway through...none of my progress was saved."
Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/freeletics-workouts-fitness/id654810212

## Hypothesis

People who lost progress to sync failures will respond to everything living on the phone by architecture.

## Hook

Built on your phone. Nothing to lose to a sync.

## Format

static card, hook plus offline UI state (instagram)

## Pre-registered success signal

saves per 1,000 impressions beats the angle-bank median over 7 days

## Kill criterion

bottom-quartile saves per 1,000 impressions across two consecutive 7-day reads

## Disclosure

Screen images simulated. App is pre-release.
