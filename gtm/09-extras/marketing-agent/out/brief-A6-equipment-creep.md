## Pain state

Home programs quietly demand large gym equipment you do not have.
Verbatim source quote: "Sometimes they used large gym equipment not typical for a home"
Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/centr-strength-fitness-app/id1382530817

## Hypothesis

People misled by equipment creep will respond to zero-equipment as a hard boundary, not a bullet.

## Hook
