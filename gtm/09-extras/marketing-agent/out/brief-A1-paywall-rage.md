# Creative brief: A1-paywall-rage

Status: draft.
This brief was generated by the dry-run creative loop.
It has never been published and has never touched a real ad account.

## Pain state

Paywall or subscription gates everything; the app will not do anything until you enter a trial that converts to paid.
Verbatim source quote: "you cannot use this app unless you pay for it. You just can't."
Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/30-day-fitness-workout-at-home/id1099771240

## Hypothesis

People burned by hard paywalls will respond to a free tier stated as an absolute with no trial trap.

## Hook

Free means the workouts. All of them. Forever.

## Format

static card, hook plus Ready Screen UI (instagram)

## Pre-registered success signal

saves per 1,000 impressions beats the angle-bank median over 7 days

## Kill criterion

bottom-quartile saves per 1,000 impressions across two consecutive 7-day reads

## Disclosure

Screen images simulated. App is pre-release.
