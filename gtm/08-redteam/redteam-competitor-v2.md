## Objections

### 1. MUST-FIX - the site refuses the demand the whole channel plan depends on capturing

Target: 03-site/index.html hero aside ("Rep Today is pre-launch. No waitlist, nothing to reserve.") and FAQ "When does it launch?" ("No waitlist to join."), versus 06-channels/channel-plan.md section A4 ("the waitlist is the measurement spine of the whole plan") and section 2 ("build first signal from organic short-form content and a first-party waitlist").
The channel plan's entire paid-vs-organic verdict rests on a first-party waitlist as the one attribution-proof funnel, and A4 budgets founder hours to seed it, yet the shipped landing page explicitly tells visitors there is nothing to join.
From my seat this is the best gift in the package: every viewer a winning RepToday video warms up has nowhere to land, and I have a live App Store listing, a 30-day trial, and an always-on ad flight ready to harvest "instant bodyweight workout" intent the same week.
Smallest honest fix: either add a plain email-capture form to index.html before any posting begins, or amend channel-plan.md A4 and section 2 to state the waitlist does not yet exist and make "waitlist live on the page" a hard precondition in the pre-publication checklist.

### 2. MUST-FIX - the A1 kill criterion is keyed to a metric the package itself declares unmeasurable

Target: 06-channels/channel-plan.md section A1 kill criterion ("profile-link taps to the waitlist stay at zero"), versus 05-social-pmf/angles.md "Measurement honesty" ("NOT reliably measurable at zero followers: profile-link taps ... and no link destination exists pre-launch").
The stop/continue decision for the #1 ranked channel depends on a quantity the PMF kit's own measurement-honesty section rules out, and on a destination objection 1 shows does not exist.
As written, week 8 arrives and the criterion is either vacuously triggered (taps are zero because the link cannot exist) or silently ignored, and either way the kill rule measured nothing.
Smallest honest fix: rewrite the A1 kill criterion to use only the per-concept relative metrics angles.md certifies as measurable (median-view multiples, watched-full ranks), and reintroduce link taps only if and when a live waitlist link exists.

### 3. SURVIVING-OBJECTION - I can fake the hero experience at the creative layer within a quarter, and the package under-weights this

Target: 02-brand/positioning.md, Hero A and "What this positioning deliberately gives up"; 05-social-pmf/angles.md P01, P06, P15.
Positioning.md already concedes "perceived instant-open can be faked with caching," and that concession is the whole counter-playbook: I ship a "Today's pick, ready when you open" card that server-caches one recommended class overnight, then run the identical screen-record format (cold open, one Start button, tap counter) with an instructor's face on it.
A scroller cannot distinguish deterministic on-device generation from a cached recommendation in 8 seconds, so P01, P06, and P15 are copyable at the only layer the audience sees, and my version carries "4.9 stars, 813K ratings" where RepToday's carries "not released yet."
Being honest both ways: I cannot copy free-forever (we closed our free tier in April 2024 and our CFO called it "cannibalizing" on the record) and I cannot copy no-streaks (Club Peloton's streaks, badges, and Bronze-to-Legend levels are load-bearing retention machinery; ripping them out to counter a zero-user app is a board-level fight I would lose).
The package names partial imitability in one line of the give-ups section; it should instead rank angles by copy-resistance and put the structurally uncopyable claims (free-forever, forgiveness) ahead of the fakeable ones in the test order.

### 4. SURVIVING-OBJECTION - months of "not released yet" posting is a free R&D window for me

Target: 05-social-pmf/week-1-drafts.md (every end card: "iOS. Not released yet."), 06-channels/channel-plan.md section 1c and A1 (a quarter-long cadence, 20-30 posts per platform, launch as "one more beat").
The plan runs a public, pre-registered message tournament for 8-plus weeks before a listing exists, with every asset labeled pre-launch.
Whatever concept wins, wins in public: my team reads the same TikTok analytics signals (comments, shares, format) that RepToday does, and I can have a matching creative in the auction before RepToday can accept its first install.
The unoccupied territories the sweep found (ready-on-open, anti-streak kindness) are unoccupied for me too, and this cadence hands me the validated hook language to occupy them first with a live product.
This tradeoff may still be right for a zero-audience founder, but the plan should carry it visibly: shorten the public-testing window once a winner emerges, and treat time-to-listing as a competitive variable, not a background task.

### 5. SURVIVING-OBJECTION - the package has no answer to the instructor counterattack, which is the counter I would actually run

Target: 02-brand/positioning.md messaging hierarchy; 05-social-pmf/angles.md (all 16 rows); 03-site/index.html FAQ.
My cheapest counter is not to contest friction at all but to reframe the category: "a list of push-ups from an algorithm, or a coach who knows your name" - instructor charisma is my verified top review strength and my ad flight already sells it ("instructors who breathe life into every rep").
Nothing in the positioning, the angle bank, or the site FAQ addresses why no-instructor and no-video-guidance is a feature rather than a poverty signal, so the first time this frame lands in RepToday's comments, the founder is improvising the answer on camera.
The honest version of the answer exists in the product facts (guidance without a performance to keep up with, sessions that work at 9pm with the sound off) but it is currently written nowhere.
Carry this visibly: add the no-instructor question to the site FAQ and hold a drafted, guardrail-compliant reply for comments.

### 6. NOTE - the day-2 paywall review is six years old

Target: 05-social-pmf/week-1-drafts.md, Day 2, shot 1 attribution ("Real App Store review of a fitness app, 2020").
The attribution is honest, but a hostile commenter (or my community team) will point out the founder's flagship evidence of paywall rage predates the product by six years.
A fresher verbatim quote from the same mined corpus would close the hole; pain-point-frequency.md ranks paywall rage #1, so recent material should exist.

### 7. NOTE - the P15 tap-count claim invites bad-faith counting

Target: 05-social-pmf/angles.md P15 and week-1-drafts.md Day 1 shot list.
"Taps between opening this app and your first rep: one" is technically precise (the icon tap is the opening, Start is the one tap), but the demo visibly shows two thumb taps, and comment sections do not read carefully.
I would seed exactly that comment.
The on-screen counter should visibly start at zero after the app is open, or the hook should say "one tap after it opens."

## What I could not break

The forgiveness mechanic (P02, P03, P13) and the free-forever wedge (P07) are structurally defended against Peloton specifically, for the reasons in objection 3, and the claims-hygiene rules held everywhere I checked: no invented numbers, no unfetched citations, "most" never "every," the NTC carve-out honestly applied, competitor-motive claims kept internal.
The weakest angle from my seat is P06 (on-device speed): the sub-100ms claim is imperceptible, unverifiable by a viewer, and matched perceptually by any app with a warm cache, so it tests a spec where every other angle tests a felt difference.
What would make my counter hardest, in order: fix objections 1 and 2 so the message converts somewhere I cannot reach, lead with the compound stack (instant AND offline AND free AND question-free at once, which positioning.md itself calls the durable claim) instead of single mechanics I can fake one at a time, and get the listing live before the public tournament crowns a winner for me.
