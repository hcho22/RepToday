# Competitor Teardown: Apple Fitness+

Research date: 2026-07-15 (all fetches 2026-07-15T04:18Z to 04:21Z UTC).
All factual claims below are sourced from pages fetched live during this run.
Prices and features reflect what the fetched pages showed on that date.

## TL;DR

Apple Fitness+ is a subscription video-workout catalog (trainer-led classes, 5-45 min) inside Apple's free Fitness app, positioned as "Gets you moving. Keeps you going." at $9.99/mo or $79.99/yr ([apple.com/apple-fitness-plus](https://www.apple.com/apple-fitness-plus/)).
It is a browse-and-choose library, not a ready-on-open session: users pick from a catalog or set up a multi-step Custom Plan (days, time per day, plan length, up to 5 activity types, trainers, music, start date) ([support.apple.com Custom Plans guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/fitness-plus/use-custom-plans-apdf222051d8/ios)).
The surrounding Apple Watch Activity system is explicitly streak-and-award based ([Apple Watch activity guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/guide/watch/apd3bf6d85a6/watchos)).
The Fitness app's App Store rating was 2.9/5 across 11,000+ ratings on the fetched page, with complaints centered on glitches, navigation, and plan tracking ([App Store page](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/apple-fitness/id1208224953)).
Rep Today's wedge: instant deterministic session on open, zero equipment always, forgiving non-streak Consistency Score, and no awards/badges of any kind.

## 1. What it is and positioning

Apple Fitness+ is Apple's subscription fitness service: a catalog of trainer-led video workouts and meditations across 12 types (Strength, HIIT, Kickboxing, Yoga, Rowing, Pilates, Meditation, Cycling, Treadmill Run, Dance, Core, Mindful Cooldown), 5-45 minutes each, with 30+ trainers and new content weekly ([apple.com/apple-fitness-plus](https://www.apple.com/apple-fitness-plus/)).
The hero message on the fetched marketing page: "Gets you moving. Keeps you going." - a motivation-and-momentum framing built around trainers, video production, and Apple Watch integration ([same page](https://www.apple.com/apple-fitness-plus/)).
It lives inside the free Apple Fitness app (developer: Apple Inc.) ([App Store page](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/apple-fitness/id1208224953)).

## 2. Pricing and free-tier shape

Per the fetched marketing page (2026-07-15): $9.99/month or $79.99/year, 1 month free for new subscribers, 3 months free with an eligible device purchase, and available in the Apple One bundle ([apple.com/apple-fitness-plus](https://www.apple.com/apple-fitness-plus/)).
The Fitness app itself is free and does activity tracking (rings, workout metrics), but all Fitness+ workout content sits behind the subscription once the trial ends - there is no free workout tier ([apple.com/apple-fitness-plus](https://www.apple.com/apple-fitness-plus/); [App Store page](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/apple-fitness/id1208224953)).

## 3. Onboarding and session-start friction

Fitness+ does not open to a ready workout; discovery is browse, search, or the personalized "For You" recommendations section ([Fitness+ at-a-glance guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/fitness-plus/apple-fitness-at-a-glance-apd177916f2d/ios)).
The marketing page says users select preferred activities at signup to seed "For You" recommendations ([apple.com/apple-fitness-plus](https://www.apple.com/apple-fitness-plus/)).
Its structured offering, Custom Plans, requires an explicit multi-step setup before any plan workout exists: tap the days you want to work out, tap a duration next to "Total Time Per Day", tap a duration next to "Length of Plan", tap up to 5 activities, optionally set trainer and music preferences, pick a start date, then "Tap Create Plan" ([Custom Plans guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/fitness-plus/use-custom-plans-apdf222051d8/ios)).
That is exactly the "how long do you have / what do you want" interrogation Rep Today refuses to put in front of Start.
The fetched Custom Plans guide says nothing about a forgiveness mechanic for missed workouts; it only notes you can "go back to previous days or weeks to complete workouts at any time during your plan" ([same guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/fitness-plus/use-custom-plans-apdf222051d8/ios)).

## 4. Equipment and offline

Apple Watch is not required; minimum is an iPhone 8 or later on iOS 16.1+, with iPad and Apple TV 4K also supported and Watch as an optional metrics layer ([Fitness+ at-a-glance guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/fitness-plus/apple-fitness-at-a-glance-apd177916f2d/ios)).
The marketing page states most workouts require no equipment, but some need dumbbells, resistance bands, an indoor bike, treadmill, or rowing machine ([apple.com/apple-fitness-plus](https://www.apple.com/apple-fitness-plus/)).
Offline exists but as manual pre-downloading: users can "download workouts and meditations for staying active offline", with the limitation that "The Burn Bar isn't available during downloaded workouts" ([save-and-download guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/fitness-plus/save-and-download-to-your-library-apd61ed9dc0d/ios)).
Nothing in the fetched pages suggests workouts can be generated offline - it is a streamed/downloaded video catalog.

## 5. Gamification

The surrounding Activity/Fitness ecosystem is award-driven: Apple's Watch guide says you can "earn awards for personal records, streaks, and major milestones", browsable by category including Monthly Challenges and "Activity Competition awards" earned by competing with friends ([Apple Watch activity guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/guide/watch/apd3bf6d85a6/watchos)).
The Fitness+ marketing page also promotes awards for accomplishments (e.g., 100 completed workouts), Activity ring syncing, and shared progress via Family Sharing ([apple.com/apple-fitness-plus](https://www.apple.com/apple-fitness-plus/)).
So: streaks, badges/awards, monthly challenges, and friend competitions are core - the opposite of Rep Today's no-XP/no-badge stance.

## 6. Review themes (from the fetched App Store page)

The Apple Fitness app showed a 2.9/5 rating across 11,000+ ratings on the fetched App Store page ([App Store page](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/apple-fitness/id1208224953)).
Praise themes in visible reviews: trainers, Apple TV/Watch integration, and motivation (one 4-star reviewer had completed 100+ workouts).
Complaint themes: playback glitches, weak navigation/filtering, and plan tracking failures.
Verbatim snippets from that fetched page:

- "it would just freeze then I would have to restart the whole thing" (review titled "Like the workout when they actually work", July 2022).
- "I absolutely hate scrolling down to the bottom to get to my saved workouts" (review titled "Fitness + pros and cons", August 2023).
- "I do get frustrated when I do a workout...but it does not 'check' off of my plan" (review titled "Motivating app, glitches are frustrating", March 2024).

## 7. Wedge contrast: Rep Today vs Apple Fitness+

- **Ready-on-open vs browse-and-choose.** Fitness+ starts with a catalog, search, and "For You" recommendations ([at-a-glance guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/fitness-plus/apple-fitness-at-a-glance-apd177916f2d/ios)); its Custom Plans need an 8-step setup wizard before day one ([Custom Plans guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/fitness-plus/use-custom-plans-apdf222051d8/ios)). Rep Today opens to a complete session with one Start button and never asks questions first.
- **Offline by architecture vs offline by pre-download.** Fitness+ offline means remembering to download videos in advance, with features degraded ("The Burn Bar isn't available during downloaded workouts") ([download guide](https://support.apple.com/guide/fitness-plus/save-and-download-to-your-library-apd61ed9dc0d/ios)). Rep Today generates every session on-device, deterministically, fully offline.
- **Zero equipment always vs mostly-no-equipment.** Fitness+ includes workouts needing dumbbells, bikes, treadmills, and rowers ([apple.com/apple-fitness-plus](https://www.apple.com/apple-fitness-plus/)). Every Rep Today movement passes the hotel-room test.
- **Forgiving Consistency Score vs streaks, awards, and competitions.** Apple's ecosystem rewards streaks, personal records, Monthly Challenges, and friend competitions ([Watch activity guide](https://support.apple.com/en-us/guide/watch/apd3bf6d85a6/watchos)). Rep Today has no streaks, XP, levels, badges, or leaderboards; a miss dents the score but never zeroes it, and returns are celebrated.
- **Free core loop vs no free tier.** All Fitness+ workout content is paid after trial at $9.99/mo ([apple.com/apple-fitness-plus](https://www.apple.com/apple-fitness-plus/)); Rep Today's workouts are free and unlimited forever, with premium (~$7.99/mo) unlocking depth only.

[ASSUMPTION] Overlap risk is moderate rather than head-on: Fitness+ sells trainer-led video classes to people who want to follow along, while Rep Today sells a zero-decision daily rep habit; the shared audience is busy iPhone owners wanting short (5+ min) home workouts. Reasoning: both offer 5-minute-minimum home sessions per the fetched pages, but the fetched content shows no Fitness+ mechanic that generates a session instantly without choosing.
