# Competitor Teardown: Sweat (by Kayla Itsines)

**TL;DR:** Sweat is a women-only, community-and-trainer-led program app (BBG lineage) with a questionnaire-first setup, browse-based workout selection, trophy-badge gamification, and a subscription-only model (7-day trial, no free tier).
It competes on trainer personality and community, not on friction - which leaves Rep Today's ready-on-open, free-core, no-gamification wedge untouched.

## What it is and positioning

Sweat positions itself as training "at home or in the gym" inside "the world's largest women's fitness community," fronted by trainers (Kayla Itsines, Kelsey Wells, and others).
The sweat.com hero message is "A more empowered you starts with Sweat," explicitly built by and for women (https://www.sweat.com/, fetched 2026-07-15).
The App Store listing is "Sweat: Fitness App For Women," subtitle "Workout At Home Or In The Gym," Editors' Choice, 4.6 stars across roughly 43K ratings as shown on the fetched page (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sweat-fitness-app-for-women/id1049234587, fetched 2026-07-15).

## Pricing and free tier

- App Store in-app purchase list (fetched 2026-07-15): monthly tiers shown from $13.99 to $24.99, quarterly $54.99, yearly $134.99, plus a $19.99 Kayla-branded subscription (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sweat-fitness-app-for-women/id1049234587).
- A third-party review (Treadmill Review Guru) lists $19.99/mo, $119.94/yr, and the 7-day free trial (https://www.treadmillreviewguru.com/sweat-app-review/, fetched 2026-07-15). Prices on the two fetched pages differ; both are reported as-shown, dated.
- There is no permanent free tier: the homepage funnels everything into the 7-day trial (https://www.sweat.com/), and a top App Store review complains that canceling loses all access and history.

## Onboarding and session-start friction

Setup is a questionnaire: fitness goals, fitness level, where you want to train, how you want to train, and preferred training styles (https://www.treadmillreviewguru.com/sweat-app-review/).
After onboarding, workouts are chosen by browsing: multi-week structured programs or an "On Demand" tab - the user picks; the app does not open to a ready session.
Program weeks start only on Mondays and rest timers are fixed, both of which show up as user complaints (see below).

## Equipment assumptions

The listing advertises options with "no equipment, free weights or machines," and setup asks what equipment you have so the algorithm filters compatible workouts (App Store listing; Treadmill Review Guru).
In practice the flagship programs (PWR, Build) are weights-forward; zero-equipment is a filter, not the product's identity.

## Gamification

Trophy badges earned through program challenges, weekly challenges, global "Sweat Challenge" events, an in-app community forum, and "sweaty selfies" sharing (App Store listing; https://www.treadmillreviewguru.com/sweat-app-review/).
Community accountability is the core retention mechanic.

## Review themes (from fetched review pages)

Praise: workout quality and variety, intuitive interface with timers and substitutions, 30-40 minute workouts fitting busy schedules, community motivation (https://apps.apple.com/us/app/sweat-fitness-app-for-women/id1049234587?see-all=reviews, fetched 2026-07-15).
Complaints, verbatim from that fetched review page:

> "You do not get too keep the workouts like you do in the PDF version. If you cancel your subscription, all access is gone." - SxyPizza

> "you have to keep it open for your entire workout which totally drains my phone's battery" - KHavey

> "Then you get to week 9 and especially week 10 and it pushes you way harder" - Bisqraysgirl

Other complaint themes on the fetched page: inflexible scheduling (weeks start Mondays, fixed rest times), abrupt difficulty jumps mid-program, and subscription-model resentment.

## Wedge contrast vs Rep Today

- **Questionnaire-first, browse-based** vs Rep Today opening to a ready session with one Start button and no questions.
- **Difficulty jumps mid-program** (the week 9-10 complaint) vs Rep Today's Asymmetric Ramp that backs off immediately when a session felt too hard.
- **Trophy badges, challenges, community pressure** vs no XP/levels/badges and a forgiving, non-streak Consistency Score.
- **Subscription-only with access loss on cancel** vs a free tier with unlimited workouts forever.
- **Women-only community positioning with trainer personalities** vs a quiet, neutral, single-player tool; Sweat's audience overlap with Rep Today's tired desk worker is partial at best - it competes hardest where Rep Today does not play (community-driven program followers).
