# Creator and Community Landscape for Rep Today

Research run: 2026-07-15, all fetches between 04:18Z and 04:28Z.
Method note: youtube.com channel pages truncated and socialblade.com returned 403 during this run, so all YouTube subscriber figures below come from socialcounts.org live-count pages (channel IDs resolved and verified against each channel's own YouTube HTML), fetched 2026-07-15.

## TL;DR

The bodyweight/movement creator space around Rep Today's audience is large (eight relevant channels totaling roughly 18M YouTube subscribers as fetched 2026-07-15) and splits into three clusters: movement-lifestyle (Strength Side, GMB, Tom Merrick), shame-free beginner calisthenics (Hybrid Calisthenics), and pain/rehab (Squat University, MoveU, Conor Harris), with The Bioneer as a functional-training essayist.
The best zero-cost fit is participation-first posting in r/bodyweightfitness and r/fitness30plus, and the best partnership fits are GMB Fitness (explicitly targets busy people training 15-45 minutes) and, tonally, Hybrid Calisthenics.
Critical caveat: reddit.com and every mirror I tried were blocked during this run, so subreddit self-promotion rules could NOT be verified and must be read manually before posting anything.

## Anchor: Strength Side

Strength Side is a movement/mobility YouTube channel showing 2,286,682 subscribers, 846 videos, and 326,036,767 total views, with the channel description "Movement as a catalyst for physical and mental transformation" ([socialcounts.org](https://socialcounts.org/youtube-live-subscriber-count/UC9BNGVGr_lvbn3d23ckuVOA), fetched 2026-07-15 ~04:19Z).
Their site sells programs including "The Human Animal Method" ($150), "Move Strong Now" ($209), and "Master Mobility" ($150), plus subscription on-demand classes, under the tagline "Reclaim the way your body was built to move" ([strengthside.com](https://strengthside.com/), fetched 2026-07-15 ~04:27Z).
The site shows no partnership or sponsorship page, only a generic contact page and social links (same fetch).
Their "natural movement" blend of mobility, strength, and flexibility overlaps directly with Rep Today's three pillars, including primal movement.

## Adjacent creator map (all subscriber counts from socialcounts.org, fetched 2026-07-15 ~04:22Z)

- **Squat University** - 6,791,696 subs, 4,725 videos; run by physical therapist Dr. Aaron Horschig; "clinical masterclass on how to fix back pain, improve mobility and bulletproof your body for the gym" ([source](https://socialcounts.org/youtube-live-subscriber-count/UCyPYQTT20IgzVw92LDvtClw)). Rehab/barbell-adjacent, partial audience fit.
- **Hybrid Calisthenics** - 4,469,187 subs, 774 videos; Hampton Liu's goal is "to help people cultivate long-term fitness and health with calisthenics" ([source](https://socialcounts.org/youtube-live-subscriber-count/UCeJFgNahi--FKs0oJyeRDEw)). His about page says "we don't need people to sell us our own health, happiness, and fitness" and his course and routine are "currently free" ([hybridcalisthenics.com/about](https://www.hybridcalisthenics.com/about), fetched ~04:27Z). Newsweek (2021-05-13) covered him being praised for removing shame from training, quoting "There's no reason to be ashamed if you can't do a push-up" ([newsweek.com](https://www.newsweek.com/wholesome-fitness-influencer-praised-removing-shame-personal-training-1591196), fetched ~04:28Z).
- **Tom Merrick (Bodyweight Warrior)** - 1,155,671 subs, 680 videos; "Honest and practical guidance for developing bodyweight strength, flexibility, handstands and more" ([source](https://socialcounts.org/youtube-live-subscriber-count/UCU0DZhN-8KFLYO6beSaYljg)). His site claims "over 1 million in the tribe" and lists his own mobile app, membership, and coaching ([bodyweightwarrior.co.uk/about](https://www.bodyweightwarrior.co.uk/about/), fetched ~04:28Z) - his own app makes him a quasi-competitor.
- **Conor Harris** - 1,032,484 subs, 968 videos; "pain management, health, performance, and fitness" via biomechanics ([source](https://socialcounts.org/youtube-live-subscriber-count/UC634L9eG-YsskyzWxnp9BJA)). Pain-education focus, weaker fit.
- **The Bioneer** - 951,919 subs, 672 videos; Adam Sinicki's video essays on "human performance," functional training, and brain training ([source](https://socialcounts.org/youtube-live-subscriber-count/UCIh_TPYPqjJuS_-nOfAIlfg)). Essay format means a novel product angle (deterministic on-device generation, no gamification) could be topic material rather than a paid placement.
- **MoveU** - 847,797 subs, 1,960 videos; a chronic-pain methodology company selling "real coaching, real programming, a step-by-step system" ([source](https://socialcounts.org/youtube-live-subscriber-count/UCfqzZR7tG1GufD-D7EofBaQ)). A program-selling company, not a partner candidate.
- **GMB Fitness** - 370,987 subs, 333 videos; run by Ryan Hurst, movement and pain-relief focus, "no gym equipment" ([source](https://socialcounts.org/youtube-live-subscriber-count/UC_ruB7qtdk4KufASPRuWhZA)). Their site claims "over 123,019+ clients in over 100 countries" and targets "people who don't want to feel limited physically but have more important things to do than spend hours every day working out," with results in "15-45 minutes, a few times a week" ([gmb.io](https://gmb.io/), fetched ~04:27Z) - nearly Rep Today's exact positioning.

## Communities: subreddits

Member counts conflict between the two sources I could fetch, so both are shown.

- **r/bodyweightfitness**: gummysearch shows 4.8M members, +101k (2.2%) in the past year ([gummysearch.com](https://gummysearch.com/r/bodyweightfitness/), fetched ~04:26Z); frontpagemetrics shows 2,534,639 subscribers but its milestone log ends Feb 2021, so it appears stale ([frontpagemetrics.com](https://frontpagemetrics.com/r/bodyweightfitness), fetched ~04:24Z). Self-description: "redditors who like to use their own body to train" (both fetches). Common threads are advice and solution requests, and fitness apps are among frequently discussed products (gummysearch fetch).
- **r/flexibility**: gummysearch shows 2.4M members, +57k (2.4%) per year ([gummysearch.com](https://gummysearch.com/r/flexibility/), fetched ~04:27Z); frontpagemetrics shows 214,147 with milestones ending Sep 2020, again likely stale ([frontpagemetrics.com](https://frontpagemetrics.com/r/flexibility), fetched ~04:25Z).
- **r/fitness30plus**: 197,000 members, +21k (11.9%) in the past year - the fastest-growing of the set - self-described as "a sub for discussing fitness for people over 30" with "struggling" and "progress" as top discussion themes ([gummysearch.com](https://gummysearch.com/r/fitness30plus/), fetched ~04:26Z). This is Rep Today's tired-parent-at-9pm audience almost verbatim.
- **r/getdisciplined**: 665,920 subscribers per frontpagemetrics (milestones end Sep 2020, treat as stale/lower bound), "Help others attain self-discipline, by sharing what helps you" ([frontpagemetrics.com](https://frontpagemetrics.com/r/getdisciplined), fetched ~04:26Z). Relevant to the discipline-first framing rather than the workout content itself.

## What could NOT be verified: self-promotion rules

reddit.com, old.reddit.com, api.reddit.com, web.archive.org, and six redlib/libreddit mirror instances were all blocked or unreachable from this environment during this run.
Therefore no subreddit's self-promotion, app-promotion, or survey rules were verified, and none are stated here.
[ASSUMPTION] Large fitness subreddits typically restrict app promotion heavily; reasoning: gummysearch's fetched pages show these communities are dominated by advice threads, not product posts, and major subreddits of this size generally have anti-spam moderation. Treat every subreddit as no-promotion until a human reads the actual rules pages before launch.

## Ranked plausible zero-cost or partnership channels (solo pre-launch founder)

1. **r/fitness30plus** (participate-first posting) - smallest but fastest-growing and the closest audience match; "struggling" and "progress" thread culture suits founder-as-member participation. Rules unverified; read them first.
2. **r/bodyweightfitness** (participate-first posting) - the audience bullseye for zero-equipment training at multi-million scale; fitness apps are already a discussed product category there. Rules unverified; highest moderation risk. [ASSUMPTION].
3. **GMB Fitness** (partnership outreach) - verified positioning overlap (busy people, no equipment, 15-45 minutes); realistic ask is a conversation or mention, not promotion, since they sell their own programs.
4. **Hybrid Calisthenics** (tone-aligned long shot) - the shame-free, forgiving philosophy matches Rep Today's non-streak Consistency Score exactly, but his verified anti-selling stance makes promoting a paid-tier app unlikely; free-tier framing is the only viable pitch.
5. **The Bioneer** (story pitch) - the deterministic no-AI-workout, no-gamification design is essay material for a channel about training ideas.
6. **Strength Side** (cold outreach, low probability) - anchor-audience fit via the primal/mobility pillars, but no partnership surface found on their site and they sell $150+ competing programs.
7. **r/flexibility and r/getdisciplined** (secondary participation) - relevant to the mobility pillar and discipline framing respectively.
8. **Squat University, MoveU, Conor Harris** (deprioritize) - pain/rehab positioning and, for MoveU, a competing paid methodology; poor fit for a discipline-first micro-workout app.
