# Competitor Teardown: Ladder (LADDER Strength Training Plans)

Research date: 2026-07-15 (all fetches 2026-07-15T04:18-04:20Z UTC).
All claims below are from pages fetched live during this run; prices and features are as shown on those pages on that date.

## TL;DR

Ladder is a premium, coach-led, subscription-only strength training app with no free tier beyond a 7-day trial ([apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ladder-strength-training-plans/id1502936453), [joinladder.com/pricing](https://www.joinladder.com/pricing)).
It sells human-programmed weekly plans, in-ear coaching, and team community, priced at $29.99/mo or $179.99/yr for Pro, with higher tiers up to $479.99/yr shown as in-app purchases ([apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ladder-strength-training-plans/id1502936453)).
Onboarding routes users through a team-matching quiz before they land on a plan ([joinladder.com](https://www.joinladder.com/), [joinladder.com/pricing](https://www.joinladder.com/pricing)).
It is equipment-forward (dumbbells, barbells, kettlebells, cables, plus bodyweight options) and gym/home oriented ([apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ladder-strength-training-plans/id1502936453)).
It occupies almost the opposite corner of the market from Rep Today: paid-only, coach-and-community-driven, equipment-assuming, 30-40 minute sessions, versus Rep Today's free-core, zero-equipment, ready-on-open micro-sessions.

## 1. What it is and positioning

Ladder (App Store name "LADDER Strength Training Plans", subtitle "Fitness, Workouts, Coaching") positions itself as "the #1 app for strength training" built on coach-created "structured progressive overload programs" ([apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ladder-strength-training-plans/id1502936453)).
The website's hero at fetch time was celebrity-led ("Hilary Duff Trains on Ladder") with the promise "Know exactly what workout to do, every day" - a plan created by expert coaches and updated weekly ([joinladder.com](https://www.joinladder.com/)).
It targets busy people who want results in less time via structured programming rather than random workout content ([joinladder.com](https://www.joinladder.com/)).
The App Store page showed a 4.9/5 rating from 163K ratings and an Editors' Choice award; the site also claims App Store App of the Year 2025 Finalist ([apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ladder-strength-training-plans/id1502936453), [joinladder.com](https://www.joinladder.com/)).

## 2. Pricing and free-tier shape

There is no permanent free tier; only a 7-day free trial that does not require a credit card ([joinladder.com/pricing](https://www.joinladder.com/pricing)).
Pricing page (fetched 2026-07-15): Pro Monthly $29.99/mo (1 team, changeable 2x monthly); Pro Annual $179.99/yr ($14.99/mo equivalent) adding all-teams access, unlimited team changes, and unlimited Save & Replay workouts ([joinladder.com/pricing](https://www.joinladder.com/pricing)).
The App Store in-app purchase list showed additional tiers: PRO $29.99/mo or $179.99/yr, PRO+ $34.99/mo or $329.99/yr, ELITE $44.99/mo or $449.99/yr, ELITE+ $49.99/mo or $479.99/yr ([apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ladder-strength-training-plans/id1502936453)).
A third-party reviewer described the Elite difference as including "1-1 coaching" versus Pro's "general coaching classes", and stated flatly: "There's no real free version - you have to pay to fully use the app (aside from the 7-day trial)" ([outdoorsynomad.com](https://www.outdoorsynomad.com/ladder-fitness-app-review/)).

## 3. Onboarding and session-start friction

Ladder's onboarding is a matching flow: users take a "team quiz" to receive personalized coach/program recommendations based on goals and training preferences, then join a team whose plan drops weekly ([joinladder.com](https://www.joinladder.com/), [joinladder.com/pricing](https://www.joinladder.com/pricing)).
The pricing page mentions "personalized plan recommendations" and "introductory welcome workouts", and trial users can sample multiple teams ([joinladder.com/pricing](https://www.joinladder.com/pricing)).
Once inside a team, the model is "a new workout plan every day", programmed by the coach and refreshed weekly, with workouts described as 30-40 minutes ([joinladder.com](https://www.joinladder.com/)).
So the daily session is pre-decided by the coach rather than by a question flow, but reaching that state requires quiz, team selection, and subscription; no fetched page describes an instant open-to-Start experience, and sessions are fixed-length coach programming rather than user-time-adaptive.
[ASSUMPTION] Session length flexibility appears limited to swapping exercises/sessions rather than regenerating to a chosen duration, based on the swap features described by the third-party review; no fetched page shows a duration picker.

## 4. Equipment and offline

Workouts are "designed for home or gym" with equipment categories of bodyweight, cables, dumbbells, barbells, and kettlebells ([apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ladder-strength-training-plans/id1502936453)).
Equipment varies by program; some teams are dumbbell-only, with kettlebell and minimal-equipment options ([joinladder.com](https://www.joinladder.com/)).
The app "lists the type of equipment you'll need, so you can prep in advance or choose a bodyweight workout" ([outdoorsynomad.com](https://www.outdoorsynomad.com/ladder-fitness-app-review/)).
Offline: per the same third-party review, "Ladder lets you save workouts for offline use, so you can train even without Wi-Fi or service" - i.e. download-for-offline, not offline generation; Ladder's own fetched pages did not mention offline ([outdoorsynomad.com](https://www.outdoorsynomad.com/ladder-fitness-app-review/)).

## 5. Gamification

Ladder's engagement layer is community-first: team group chat, a "Cheers" encouragement button, a selfie wall, and progress tracking via the Ladder Journal with rep/weight logging and estimated one-rep max ([apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ladder-strength-training-plans/id1502936453), [outdoorsynomad.com](https://www.outdoorsynomad.com/ladder-fitness-app-review/)).
It does use badges: "The app uses seasonal challenges and personalized milestones to award you a badge" ([outdoorsynomad.com](https://www.outdoorsynomad.com/ladder-fitness-app-review/)).
Workout streak badges are referenced in user testimonials on Ladder's site ([joinladder.com](https://www.joinladder.com/)).
No XP, levels, or leaderboards were mentioned on any fetched page.
A recent update (v4.10.1, July 8) added workout plateau detection alerts ([apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ladder-strength-training-plans/id1502936453)).

## 6. Review themes

From the App Store reviews page fetched 2026-07-15 ([apps.apple.com reviews](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ladder-strength-training-plans/id1502936453?see-all=reviews)):

Strengths users praise:
- Responsive human coaches and progressive programming: "My coach listens to our opinions and concerns and will adjust the workouts as we go if she's got enough feedback from everyone." - smash1018 ([apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ladder-strength-training-plans/id1502936453)).
- Team community and accountability (team chat, cross-team nutrition discussions) as the reason home workouts finally stuck.
- Value versus a personal trainer (one reviewer framed ~$15/mo annual against $60+ per trainer session).

Complaints (Apple's surfaced reviews skew positive; complaints below are from the fetched third-party review, [outdoorsynomad.com](https://www.outdoorsynomad.com/ladder-fitness-app-review/)):
- No free version and an expensive subscription.
- iOS-only, excluding Android users.
- Pro Monthly restrictions: team switching "only twice a month" and limited workout replays (three repeats before loss) unless on annual.

## 7. Wedge contrast: Rep Today vs Ladder

- Free core vs paid-only: Ladder has zero free tier after 7 days ([joinladder.com/pricing](https://www.joinladder.com/pricing)); Rep Today's core loop (unlimited workouts) is free forever, with ~$7.99/mo premium for depth only.
- Ready-on-open vs quiz-and-team funnel: Ladder requires a matching quiz, team choice, and subscription before its coach-programmed plan begins ([joinladder.com](https://www.joinladder.com/)); Rep Today opens to a fully generated session with one Start button and never asks questions before Start.
- Zero equipment vs equipment-forward: Ladder programs around dumbbells, barbells, kettlebells, and cables with bodyweight as one option among several ([apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ladder-strength-training-plans/id1502936453)); every Rep Today movement passes the hotel-room test (floor and wall only).
- Offline generation vs offline playback: Ladder's offline story is downloading coach-made workouts in advance ([outdoorsynomad.com](https://www.outdoorsynomad.com/ladder-fitness-app-review/)); Rep Today generates every session on-device, deterministically, offline, in under 100ms.
- No badges vs badges/streak-badges plus paid community: Ladder awards challenge and milestone badges and leans on team chat and cheers for retention ([outdoorsynomad.com](https://www.outdoorsynomad.com/ladder-fitness-app-review/), [apps.apple.com](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/ladder-strength-training-plans/id1502936453)); Rep Today has no XP, levels, badges, or leaderboards anywhere, and uses a forgiving rolling Consistency Score that a miss dents but never zeroes.
- Session shape: Ladder's site describes 30-40 minute coach-fixed workouts ([joinladder.com](https://www.joinladder.com/)); Rep Today serves 5-60 minutes with a one-tap duration chip, and a 5-minute session counts as a full show-up.

Positioning takeaway: Ladder wins with committed lifters who want a coach, equipment, and community; Rep Today should not fight it there, and instead owns the tired-desk-worker moment Ladder's funnel (quiz, team, $180/yr, dumbbells) structurally cannot serve.
