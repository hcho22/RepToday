# Competitor Teardown: Down Dog (Yoga Buddhi Co.) - incl. HIIT | Down Dog

Research date: 2026-07-15 (all fetches 2026-07-15 UTC; prices and features as shown on pages that day).

## TL;DR

Down Dog is a family of six generated-workout apps (Yoga, HIIT, Pilates, Barre, Meditation, Prenatal Yoga) whose core promise is "A new workout every time!" - algorithmically assembled sessions customized by user-chosen settings ([source](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/yoga-down-dog/id983693694), [source](https://www.downdogapp.com/)).
It is beloved (4.9 stars, 326K US ratings on Yoga; 4.9, 48K on HIIT) but is configure-first, login-mandatory, and subscription-required after a brief trial ([source](https://www.downdogapp.com/faq)).
Rep Today's wedge is the opposite shape: ready-on-open with zero questions, no account, fully offline on-device generation, a genuinely free unlimited tier, and a non-streak Consistency Score where Down Dog surfaces a "weekly streak" stat.

## 1. What it is and positioning

Down Dog is a suite of fitness apps by Yoga Buddhi Co. with the site hero "Great fitness anywhere" (Yoga app: "Great Yoga Anywhere"), covering Yoga, Pilates, Meditation, HIIT, Barre, and Prenatal Yoga ([downdogapp.com](https://www.downdogapp.com/), fetched 2026-07-15).
The App Store subtitle for both the Yoga and HIIT apps is "A new workout every time!" ([Yoga listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/yoga-down-dog/id983693694), [HIIT listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hiit-down-dog/id1491068090)).
The Yoga app claims "over a million possible configurations" and "builds a brand-new yoga practice every time you press start - tailored to your level, focus, pace, and time" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/yoga-down-dog/id983693694)).
The HIIT app's hero: "Turn up the heat with High Intensity Interval Training. Raise that heart rate to build strength and burn fat," with 1000+ exercises and the promise "you'll never get the same workout twice" ([hiit.downdogapp.com](https://hiit.downdogapp.com/), [HIIT App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hiit-down-dog/id1491068090)).
So the positioning pillar is variety-through-generation, not discipline or habit.

## 2. Pricing and free-tier shape

Per the company FAQ, Down Dog "moved to a mandatory subscription model in September 2018": users get "a brief free trial with full access to all features, and after that a subscription is required" ([FAQ](https://www.downdogapp.com/faq), fetched 2026-07-15).
There is effectively no permanent free tier; the FAQ also states there are no lifetime subscriptions and that buying via the website is typically cheaper than in-app.
US App Store in-app purchases shown on 2026-07-15: All Access $12.99/mo or $99.99/yr (Yoga listing, plus assorted "Down Dog Membership" tiers $2.99-$7.99) ([Yoga App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/yoga-down-dog/id983693694)); the HIIT listing showed a HIIT subscription at $12.99/mo or $99.99/yr and All Access at $12.99/$99.99/$119.99 ([HIIT App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hiit-down-dog/id1491068090)).
GB listing showed £10.99/mo-tier and £79.99 annual All Access ([GB App Store](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/yoga-down-dog/id983693694)).
The homepage carried a "40% off" promo banner at fetch time ([downdogapp.com](https://www.downdogapp.com/)).
One subscription spans all six apps, "but you must be signed in with the same email you used to make the purchase" ([FAQ](https://www.downdogapp.com/faq)).

## 3. Onboarding and session-start friction

Login is mandatory before use ([FAQ](https://www.downdogapp.com/faq)).
Starting a practice requires selecting type, practice length, instruction level (Full/Default/Less/Least), music style, and voice/language ([FAQ](https://www.downdogapp.com/faq)).
The HIIT app adds more knobs: total workout length, exercise duration, recovery intervals, difficulty levels for upper body/lower body/core, music genre, coach voice, exercise exclusions, and mix presets ([HIIT App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hiit-down-dog/id1491068090)).
An independent review of the HIIT app notes "The main screen is deceptively simple" and recommends going into the pull-up settings section first, calling it where "the real power" is; it also complains there is no way to save custom workout configurations ([becomingsigma.com](https://becomingsigma.com/app-review-hiit-by-down-dog/)).
Net: there is a start button and settings persist, but the model is configure-then-generate, not ready-on-open; the generation is server/app-side per the marketing framing "every time you press start."

## 4. Equipment and offline

HIIT is bodyweight-capable by default: "keep it simple with over 1000 exercises that use just your body weight," with optional difficulty increases "by adding dumbbells or resistance bands" ([hiit.downdogapp.com](https://hiit.downdogapp.com/)).
The FAQ mentions no required equipment for any practice type ([FAQ](https://www.downdogapp.com/faq)).
Offline exists but as pre-downloaded practices: the Yoga listing says "Available offline" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/yoga-down-dog/id983693694)), and the FAQ specifies downloads happen via Account tab -> Offline Practices, mobile only, and "because of licensing restrictions, our music will not accompany saved favorites when you are offline" ([FAQ](https://www.downdogapp.com/faq)).
So offline is a cached-content feature, not offline generation.

## 5. Gamification

Light-touch stats rather than heavy gamification: the Activity tab shows "total practices, hours practiced, or your weekly streak"; users can create personal Challenges (frequency, duration, reminders) and milestones are tracked ([FAQ](https://www.downdogapp.com/faq)).
No XP, levels, badges, or leaderboards were found on the fetched site, FAQ, or App Store pages.
Notable: Down Dog does surface a streak, but only as a stat, not as loss-framed pressure per the fetched pages.

## 6. Review themes (from fetched review content only)

Strengths users praise: endless variety ("The sequence of the poses never repeats" - US reviews page), deep customization, clear video/instruction, beginner friendliness, and perceived fair pricing; a HIIT reviewer praised "the infinite number of combinations of exercises so that no two workouts are the same" ([US reviews page](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/yoga-down-dog/id983693694?see-all=reviews), [HIIT App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hiit-down-dog/id1491068090)).
Complaints found were mostly minor feature gaps, not anger:
- "You can't adjust music volume separate to the voice" ([GB App Store review](https://apps.apple.com/gb/app/yoga-down-dog/id983693694), smartymarty85, 2019).
- "I wanted to add another 15 minutes to the current practice but I couldn't" ([US reviews page](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/yoga-down-dog/id983693694?see-all=reviews)).
- No way to save custom workout configurations in HIIT; the HIIT structure initially underwhelmed one reviewer versus traditional HIIT ([becomingsigma.com](https://becomingsigma.com/app-review-hiit-by-down-dog/)).
Notably, the fetched US review set contained no pricing complaints; users treat the subscription as fair.

## 7. Wedge contrast: Rep Today vs Down Dog

- **Ready-on-open vs configure-then-press-start.** Down Dog's whole flow is choose length/level/voice/music, then generate ([FAQ](https://www.downdogapp.com/faq)); Rep Today opens to a complete session at the learned default duration with one Start button and never asks "how long do you have?" before Start.
- **No account vs mandatory login.** Down Dog requires sign-in before anything ([FAQ](https://www.downdogapp.com/faq)); Rep Today works fully without an account.
- **Offline generation vs offline caching.** Down Dog offline means pre-downloading practices, music excluded ([FAQ](https://www.downdogapp.com/faq)); Rep Today's deterministic engine generates every session on-device offline in under 100ms.
- **Free-forever core vs trial-then-pay.** Down Dog: brief trial, then subscription required, $12.99/mo or $99.99/yr All Access as fetched ([FAQ](https://www.downdogapp.com/faq), [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/yoga-down-dog/id983693694)); Rep Today keeps unlimited workouts free forever with a ~$7.99/mo premium that never gates the core loop.
- **Non-streak Consistency Score vs weekly streak stat.** Down Dog displays a "weekly streak" and user-set challenges ([FAQ](https://www.downdogapp.com/faq)); Rep Today deliberately has no streaks, XP, levels, or badges - a forgiving rolling score where a miss dents but never zeroes.
- **Caveat to respect:** Down Dog HIIT is already bodyweight-capable and beloved (4.9, 48K ratings), so "zero equipment" alone is not the wedge against it; the wedge is friction shape, honesty of the free tier, and the discipline framing.
