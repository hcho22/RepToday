1. The engine

Every session is built on your phone

A deterministic on-device engine assembles each session from a 69-movement bodyweight library, with no network call and no AI. The days run in order: Push, Pull, Legs, then a Rest Day with an optional five-minute stretch or a Train anyway button.

You sign in once when you set the app up. After that, building and playing a session never waits on a connection.

2. The session

Press Start and it plays itself

Each set counts down and flows into the next, rests end on their own, and a quiet tone marks each change. Your Trainer shows every movement.

Done, Skip, Swap, Pause and + More time are there when you want them. None of them is required.

3. The duration chip

Change one thing in one tap

Got 20 minutes instead of 10? One tap on a duration chip, anywhere from 5 to 60 minutes, and the new session is there before your thumb lifts. Start is never disabled while it happens.

The app opens at the length you usually finish, so most days you change nothing.

4. The tuning

It adjusts between sessions, on your phone

Reps move in steps of five. Finish a session as written and the next one asks for a little more; rate it "Too hard" and it steps back. Clear a movement at the top of its range and the next rung of its ladder opens, up to the rungs kept for your level. The Strength Phase, which you earn, opens the rest.

The same on-device engine decides all of it.

Where AI is involved

Only in the optional Premium Coach, never in the daily session

Premium members may choose to use the AI Coach. It runs on Rep Today's servers and sends your message and your training records to OpenAI, which writes its replies. It can suggest things, such as a different session length, a swap, a reminder or a Custom Workout it writes, and nothing changes until you confirm it. It never writes the daily session, and nothing about it sits between opening the app and pressing Start.

Cooldown · The score

Missing a day never zeroes you out.

Rep Today keeps a Consistency Score. It is a forgiving rolling average of your recent weeks, not a streak. A 5-minute session counts as a full show-up. Fall short one week and your score dips. It does not reset. Come back after a week or more away and the app serves a session that is easier and winnable, and it counts the comeback as a full show-up instead of penalizing it.

You're someone who moves, not someone who owes.

There is no XP, no levels, no badges, and no leaderboards anywhere in the app.

Cooldown · About

What we mean by discipline.

There is a word this page has avoided until now: discipline.

Most fitness marketing uses it as a whip. Discipline as a 5am alarm, discipline as punishment, discipline as proof of character. We think that reading is wrong, and worse, useless at 9pm on a Tuesday when the kids are finally down and you have ten minutes and half a phone battery.

Discipline is not effort. Discipline is showing up.

Every decision between "I should work out" and "I am working out" is friction, and friction is what kills consistency.

So Rep Today deletes the decisions. Today's session is already built when you open the app. Five minutes counts as a full show-up. Missing yesterday costs you nothing today. The enemy this app is built against is not your character. It is the stack of small choices - which workout, how long, which program, is this the right one - that stands between wanting to move and moving.

Put plainly: the app is the disciplined one. It does the preparing, the deciding, the remembering, the adjusting. Every training day it lays a complete session out before you arrive, the way a friend might leave your shoes by the door. What is left for you is the only part that was ever yours to do: press Start.

You're someone who moves. The app's whole job is to make that the easiest true thing you prove today.

Cooldown · FAQ

Fair questions

What is Rep Today not?

It is not a gym program, not a personal trainer, and not a replacement for a gym. It uses no equipment at all, so there are no pull-up bars, no weights and no machines. It is the app for the days when the gym was never going to happen: a real session, sized to the time you actually have, already built.

Is there a workout every day?

Most days. After each Push, Pull and Legs cycle the app gives you a Rest Day, because rest is part of the program. On a Rest Day no session is waiting: you can take a five-minute stretch, or tap Train anyway and the next session is built.

Why are there no streaks?

A streak counts what you could lose, and losing one is a common reason people quit an app for good. Rep Today keeps a Consistency Score instead: a rolling average that dips after a short week and recovers when you return. You're someone who moves. The score's job is to reflect that, not to threaten it.

Does it need internet?

Setting the app up does: you sign in once, and that needs a connection. After that, sessions are generated, played and logged on your phone with no connection. Saving your plan and history to your account, the Premium AI Coach, purchases and deleting your account use a network when they run. None of them sits between opening the app and starting a session.

Can I build my own workout?

Not from scratch. You can change the length in one tap, ask for a different workout and swap a movement mid-session, but the app decides so you don't have to. With Premium, the Coach can write a Custom Workout when you ask, which you can edit before you accept it. If you want a fully customizable workout planner, this is honestly not that app.

Do I need an account?

Yes, one. You sign in with Apple, Google or an email code when you set the app up, so your plan and history are saved to your Rep Today account and survive a phone change. After that the app does not ask you to sign in again before a session, with or without a connection. Apple Health asks its own permission question, and the app works the same if you decline.

Will I run out of movements?

You don't run out of a movement, you climb it. Strength movements sit on ladders, and the engine starts you on the rung you have reached, opening the next one only once you have cleared it. The push-up ladder starts at the standard push-up, with a wall, incline or knee version offered if a full one is not there yet, and climbs through the wide and diamond push-up toward the archer and one-arm push-up. The squat ladder runs from a wall sit up toward the shrimp squat and pistol squat. The hardest rungs open with the Strength Phase, which you earn by showing up and clearing the foundations, never by paying.

Across a week it is the day that varies: Push, Pull, Legs, then rest. Warm-up and cooldown stretches rotate so you are not handed the same one two sessions running, and a Different workout button rebuilds today's session with other movements at the same length.

The library is 69 movements, and if what you want is a catalog to browse, this is honestly not that app. It requires no workout equipment, and the work in it has somewhere further to go.

Aren't some free tiers already good?

Yes. Nike Training Club's free tier, for one, is genuinely praised by its own reviewers (source (its App Store page)). Rep Today's free promise is aimed at the apps that put the workout itself behind a subscription sheet, not at the apps that already give theirs away. What Rep Today adds on top of free is the rest of the stack at once: already built, offline after setup, nothing to pick.

Is my data sold?

Never sold. There are no ads. Your history is stored on your phone first and saved to your Rep Today account when you are connected, and workouts are written to Apple Health only if you allow it.

Three things to be plain about. The app collects minimal anonymous usage measurement - events like session starts and completions, carrying a random per-install ID, never an email or advertising ID - and you can turn it off in Settings. The optional Premium AI Coach sends your message and your training records to OpenAI to write its replies. And the pre-launch waitlist keeps your email, your answers to its form, which Rep Today link brought you, if any, and the date you joined; the Privacy Policy's waitlist section says why and for how long. None of that is ever sold either.

Full details are in our Privacy Policy.

When does it launch?

It hasn't yet. Rep Today is pre-launch: the core loop is built and tested, but the app is not on the App Store. iOS first, iOS 17 or later, no Android at launch. The waitlist is open now - add your email and we'll tell you the day it ships - but there is no date to announce yet.
