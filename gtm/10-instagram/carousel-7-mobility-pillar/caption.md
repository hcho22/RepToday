# EVERGREEN D - "The warm-up is not filler." (mobility, chosen on purpose)

Evergreen rotation post. The mobility story, told from the shipped engine.
Not pinned; re-postable whenever the rotation calls for it.

Claim discipline for this one in particular. `positioning.md` pillar 5 frames
mobility as co-primary with strength and primal in the engine's pillar weighting,
sold as a co-equal share of every session. The shipped engine does not support
that: US-M01 removed the mobility middle block and `PillarBalance`/`PillarWeights`
with it, so `pillarWeighting` is inert (`Models/SessionPolicy.swift:37-43`) and
mobility now lives only as the warm-up and cooldown bookends
(`Services/Engine/SessionAssembly.swift:24-26`). Publishing the pillar-5 framing
would be a false claim about the product, so this carousel does not make it, the
same routing-around the folder README already records for pillar 5. What is true,
and what this post argues instead, is quality over quantity: the mobility you get is
deliberately chosen - matched to the day's lead movement, biased toward hip and
posture relief when you sit all day, and rotated for variety - not a generic warm-up
bolted on. See `claim-verification.md` for the code that settles each line and the
full reframe note (which quotes the retired pillar-5 hook in a file the frozen-hook
guard does not scope as publishable copy).

## Caption

The warm-up is not filler.

Every session opens with mobility: a stretch on the way in, and past ten minutes, a cooldown of held stretches on the way out. And it is not the same list every day. Every stretch is tagged with the movements it complements, so the session opens with one that fits the day's main pattern. The way in shifts as your training does.

If you sit six or more hours most days, tell it, and the warm-up leads with hip and posture openers first, the stretches that counter a chair. It rotates, too: what you did last time gets pushed back, and the longest-ago and never-tried stretches come first, so it is rarely the same stretch twice.

Here is the honest scope. Every session is strength-led. The stretches open and close it; they are not the main event, and they are not padding either. Each one is chosen to earn the minute it takes.

The stretch was picked on purpose. Not a generic warm-up bolted on the front, but one matched to the day, and to how you spend it.
