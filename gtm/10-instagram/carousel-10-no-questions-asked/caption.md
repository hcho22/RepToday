# EVERGREEN D - "No account. No login. No card." (the ungated entry)

Evergreen rotation post. The entry barrier that other generators put before a first
session - an account, a login, a card, a trial clock - argued as an absence: Rep Today
puts a Start button there instead. The one setup it does run is about you, not a workout
to choose, and it is disclosed rather than hidden.
Not pinned; re-postable whenever the rotation calls for it.

Claim discipline for this one in particular: it does not claim "no questionnaire". There
is a short one-time setup, and slide 4 names it plainly. What it claims is the absence of
the account / login / card / paywall gate, each checked against the shipped code.

## Caption

No account. No login. No card.

There is a Start button where other apps put a form. No email to hand over, no password to invent, no account to create. Sign in with Apple is offered and optional; decline it and the app makes its own local identifier and moves on.

Nothing to buy before you start, either. The workouts are not behind a paywall, nothing asks for a card before your first session, and there is no trial you have to start to reach one. The optional premium tier lives off on the Progress tab and adds depth rather than unlocking the workout.

It does ask about you, once. The first open has a short setup: your body basics, how fit you are now, and how long you usually have. It never asks you to choose a workout, and it does not come back.

The one prompt that is not the app's own is Apple's: on the way in, iOS asks for permission to write your finished sessions to Apple Health. Declining it changes nothing.

A sign-up wall turns a short window into an errand. This one has no wall. You show up empty-handed, and that is the point.

iOS, not released yet. Link in bio.
