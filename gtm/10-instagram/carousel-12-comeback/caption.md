# COMEBACK - "The way back is gentle." (returning after a gap)

Evergreen rotation post. The return-after-a-gap experience, in depth: the session
served easy and the gap forgiven by the score.
Not pinned; re-postable whenever the rotation calls for it.

Claim discipline for this one in particular: the "celebration" is a scoring
treatment, never a screen. Nothing in the app shows a welcome-back banner, so no
slide claims one. What is real, and what every slide is built on, is that the
comeback session is served gentler and the away weeks are excused from the score.

## Caption

The way back is gentle.

Go about seven days without finishing a session, and the one waiting when you come back is built as a comeback, not a normal day.

It meets you where you are now, not where you left off. However hard you were training before the gap, the comeback is capped to an easier level, so a strong past week can never serve you a punishing one.

The catch-up is spread out, never loaded onto day one. The first session back asks for less, not more. Whatever readjustment the time away calls for is eased in over the next few sessions, never dumped on the one you returned with.

The weeks you were gone are not counted against you. Consistency in Rep Today is a rolling average of showing up, not a run of days, and the empty weeks a comeback closes are dropped from that average, not scored as misses.

The session you come back with counts as a full week. It is not a banner and not a badge. The comeback is counted in the score instead: that one session lands as a full week, so coming back can only steady your number or rebuild it, never lower it.

The comeback matters most, so it is the session Rep Today makes the easiest.
