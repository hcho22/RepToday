## Caption

You do not pick a workout. It is on screen when the app opens.

Most workout apps put a decision in front of you before they put a workout in front of you. Which program. How long. Which of these videos. Where did I leave off.

Rep Today puts the session there instead. An engine assembles the whole thing on the phone, offline, and the app opens straight to it, with one Start button already waiting.

Being precise about "no questions", because it is the kind of line that gets stretched: it covers the path from opening the app to starting a session. No quiz, no sign-up, no paywall on that path. If you switch on Apple Health or iCloud sync, iOS asks its own permission questions. Those are optional, and they sit off that path.

5 to 60 minutes. Bodyweight, so it needs no equipment. Built on the phone, so it needs no signal.

iOS, not released yet. Link in bio.
