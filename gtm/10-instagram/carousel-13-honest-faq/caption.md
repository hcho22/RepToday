# FAQ - "Straight answers." (the honest questions)

Evergreen rotation post. The questions a wary person asks before a first install,
each answered against what the app actually does.
Not pinned; re-postable whenever the rotation calls for it.

Claim discipline for this one in particular: every answer is checked against
shipped behaviour, not the brand deck. On money, free means the workouts and
premium adds only a deeper progress view. On data, the honest picture is stated,
not softened: history stays on-device or the user's own private iCloud, and the
one shared signal is anonymous usage data that is on by default and can be turned
off. AI is deliberately not mentioned, so this post owes no AI disclosure.

## Caption

A few questions people ask, answered straight.

Is it really free? Yes. Every workout Rep Today builds is free to do. Not a trial, not a teaser, with no session locked behind a purchase.

Then what is premium for? Only a deeper look at your own progress: a richer view of how your training is balancing out over time. It never touches the workouts, and the core of the app stands on its own without it.

Do I need an account? No account, no sign-up. The app works without signing in to anything. Sign in with Apple is there if you want it, and skipping it changes nothing about the workouts.

Does it work offline? Yes. Every session is built on your phone, by your phone, the moment you open it. Nothing is fetched, so a plane, a basement, or a dead zone changes nothing.

What about my data? Your history is yours, and sharing is optional. Your workout history lives on your device and syncs only to your own private iCloud, never to us. The one thing you can share is anonymous usage data, on unless you turn it off in Settings, and counted against a random number rather than your name.

Then just start. The session is ready when you are.

iOS, not released yet. Link in bio.
