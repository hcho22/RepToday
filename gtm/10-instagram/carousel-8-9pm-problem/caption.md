# EVERGREEN E - "It is 9pm." (the 9pm window, recognition not aspiration)

Evergreen rotation post. The ICP's real day-in-life moment: the last window after
the kids are down, and the decision that loses to the couch.
Not pinned; re-postable whenever the rotation calls for it.

Claim discipline for this one in particular. The day-in-life beats (slides 1 to 3,
7) are recognition, not product claims, and are marked "not code-checkable" the same
way carousel 3's windows are. The product claims woven through (session already
built, sized to your time, one tap to re-length, offline, bodyweight, no account)
are each verified against code in `claim-verification.md`. No hotel room appears

## Caption

It is 9pm.

The kids are down. The house is quiet. You have maybe ten minutes before the couch wins. The will is there; the window is small, and it is closing.

And here is the thing: the last thing you have energy for is a decision. Not the movement, the choosing. Which app, which workout, how long, where to start. That is the part that loses to the couch.

So none of that is yours to do. Open Rep Today and a full session is already on the screen, sized to the time you have. Nothing to browse, nothing to pick, nothing to plan. If ten is wrong, one tap changes it, from 5 minutes up to 60, and the session rebuilds around the new number.

Wherever you already are is enough. Bodyweight, so it needs no equipment. Built on the phone, so it needs no signal. No account between you and the first move.

You did not need more time. You needed one less decision at the end of a long day. That is the whole idea.

iOS, not released yet. Link in bio.
