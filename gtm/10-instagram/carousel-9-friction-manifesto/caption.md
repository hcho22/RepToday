## Caption

The enemy is friction, not you.

You meant to move. Then came the questions. Which app. Which plan. How many minutes. Where you left off. Each one is a small decision, and stacked in front of a workout they are the reason it did not happen. Not the effort. The deciding.

So Rep Today's design comes down to one move: every choice between opening the app and starting a session is made in advance. What is left for you is to show up.

There is nothing to pick. The app opens straight onto a full bodyweight session, built on the phone and sized to the minutes you have, from 5 minutes up to 60. One Start button, no list to scroll.

A short session is not a compromise. The shortest one the app builds is scored exactly the same as the longest, so whether a small window is even worth using is not a decision you weigh anymore. It is.

And yesterday is not a number the score keeps. It is built from weeks, not single days, so there is no day to lose and no streak sitting there to break. A short week dents it a little, and the dent fades as the weeks after it come in. The choice to write the whole thing off never comes up.

What is left is the showing up.
