# EVERGREEN C - "The workout is the free part." (free forever, depth is the only paid thing)

Evergreen rotation post. Positioning pillar 2 (free means the workouts, forever;
premium gates only depth), argued as a position.
Not pinned; re-postable whenever the rotation calls for it.

Claim discipline for this one in particular: every sentence describes a Rep Today
mechanic. No competitor is named, and no claim is made about why any other product
priced the way it did. The mechanism of a paywall is described in the abstract on
slide 3; no company and no motive is attached to it. Premium is described honestly:
it exists, it is a subscription, and the deeper analytics view is the only thing it
adds. It never gates the core loop.

## Caption

The workout is the free part.

Every session is free, every time, with no limit on how many you generate. No account to make, and no card to enter. A workout you have to pay to reach has already chosen who gets to do it. Rep Today does not: the session is there the moment you open it, and starting it never costs anything.

There is a paid tier, and here is the honest part. One thing is paid: a deeper view. Pattern-by-pattern balance, your weekly training volume, and how your sessions have felt over time. That is what it unlocks, and it is the only thing it unlocks. The invitation to go deeper is a single quiet card on the Progress tab, never a popup and never mid-session, so it never stands between you and Start.

Free is the workout. Paid is the view. The paid tier only ever adds a deeper look at training you already did for free, and by the way it is built, it cannot sit between you and a session.

The workouts were never the paid part. Use Rep Today for as long as you like without paying anything. Premium is there if you want the deeper view, and invisible if you do not.

iOS, not released yet. Link in bio.
