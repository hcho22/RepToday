# COMING SOON - "It is coming to iOS." (the launch announcement)

Evergreen rotation post, written to stay true across the whole pre-launch window.
The announcement itself: what opens, what it needs, and an honest note that it is
not out yet.
Not pinned; re-postable until the app actually ships.

Claim discipline for this one in particular: the status lines must be true on the
day of posting. Pre-launch means no App Store listing, no date, and nothing more
optimistic than "not released yet" - so no slide names a date or promises a
tappable link. The waitlist call to action lives in this caption only and points
to the bio link, never to an in-caption link, because a caption is editable after
posting and a rendered slide is not.

## Caption

It is coming to iOS.

What opens when it does: a complete session, already on the screen. You open Rep Today and the workout is already built and waiting, with no browsing and no questions between opening it and starting.

What it asks of you: no equipment, no account, no signal. Bodyweight movements only, built on your phone with no connection, and no sign-in standing between you and your first session.

How long it takes: as much time as you have. Tell Rep Today how long you have and it sizes the whole session to fit, anywhere from a five-minute reset to a full hour.

Who it is for: the people who keep meaning to move. Busy, desk-bound, out of patience for planning a workout, and looking for one that is already decided by the time they open the app.

Where it is today: not out yet, and no countdown to it. Rep Today is an iOS app that has not been released, with no App Store page and no date to promise. This is a heads-up, not a launch clock.

When it opens, it opens ready. That is the whole idea behind Rep Today.

iOS, not released yet. Join the waitlist at the link in bio.
