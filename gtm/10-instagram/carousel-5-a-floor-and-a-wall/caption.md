# EVERGREEN B - "A floor and a wall." (the constraint as identity)

Evergreen rotation post. Zero equipment and offline generation, framed as the
whole identity rather than a feature bullet.
Not pinned; re-postable whenever the rotation calls for it.

## Caption

A floor and a wall. That is the equipment list, all of it.

Every movement in Rep Today is bodyweight. There is no version of a session that needs a band, a bar, or a bench, and nothing sneaks in later either. No bands in week three. No pull-up bar in month two. Bodyweight is the boundary the app is built inside, not the beginner setting you graduate out of.

The other half of the constraint is signal. The session is not fetched from anywhere. It is built on the phone, by the phone, every single time.

Which means the hotel-room test passes. Airplane mode on. Hotel room, second night. A basement with no bars. The session still builds.

That is not a feature, it is the whole design. A workout that needs equipment has already chosen where you will be. One that needs a server has already chosen when. This one chooses neither, so the room you happen to be standing in is never the reason the session did not happen.

The room you are already in is enough.

iOS, not released yet. Link in bio.
