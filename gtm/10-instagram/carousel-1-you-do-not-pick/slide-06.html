What "no deciding"
means here, exactly.

Setup asks about you once, on the first open. A few things, including your body basics, how fit you are now, and how long you usually have. It never asks you to choose a workout, and it does not come back.

iOS asks once, on that first open, for permission to write your sessions to Apple Health. Decline it and everything works the same. After that, the path from opening the app to starting a session asks nothing. No sign-up, no paywall.
