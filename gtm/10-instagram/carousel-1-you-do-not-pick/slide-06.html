What "no deciding"
means here, exactly.

Setup asks about you once, on the first open: how fit you are now, why you are here, how long you usually have. It never asks you to choose a workout, and it does not come back.

After that, the path from opening the app to starting a session asks nothing. No sign-up, no paywall. Apple Health and iCloud sync are optional, and iOS asks for those permissions only if you switch them on.
