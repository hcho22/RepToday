## Caption

You do not pick a workout. That part is done for you.

Wanting to move was never the hard part. The hard part is the stack of small choices in between. Which program. How long. Which of these videos. Where did I leave off.

Rep Today takes that stack off you. An engine assembles the whole session on the phone, offline, sized to the time you have, and hands it over with one Start button. Not a faster menu. No menu.

Being precise about "no deciding", because it is the kind of line that gets stretched: setup asks about you once, on the first open, and it never asks you to choose a workout. iOS asks once, on that first open, for permission to write your sessions to Apple Health, and declining changes nothing about how the app works. After that, the path from opening the app to starting a session asks nothing. No sign-up, no paywall.

5 to 60 minutes. Bodyweight, so it needs no equipment. Built on the phone, so it needs no signal.

iOS, not released yet. Link in bio.
